# AY 2026-27 Intraday Trading Income ITR Guide

Intraday gains and losses sit in their own corner of the return, and that is where most traders get stuck. The hard part is not the trading itself; it is deciding what counts as speculative business income, which expenses you can claim, how to work out turnover, and how to disclose a loss the right way. This guide is written for FY 2025-26 income that you are putting together for AY 2026-27. Treat it as a cautious filing checklist. It does not promise a refund, a processing timeline, or a shortcut around checking the official utility yourself.

## Who lands on this page

People who search for this topic are usually active intraday traders. They recognise the broad label but are not sure which documents to keep, which portal to trust, which tax records to reconcile, or what to do next. The one habit worth keeping is simple: never lift a stray answer off the internet and paste it straight into a return or an application. Begin with the official sources, then assemble a document file that someone can actually review.

For MyeCA readers, three points carry most of the weight. Keep the assessment-year or scheme-year context straight so you are not mixing up periods. Reconcile your name, PAN, Aadhaar, bank account, and income records before you submit anything. And hang on to the acknowledgement or application reference, because chasing a refund, subsidy, loan, or notice later is far harder once that reference goes missing.

## Quick checklist

- Confirm the official portal or Income Tax Department source before acting.
- Keep identity, bank, income, and scheme-specific documents in one folder.
- Match AIS, Form 26AS, Form 16, Form 16A, GST, or bank records where they apply.
- Do not assume eligibility, refund, subsidy, or loan approval without official verification.
- Use a CA or expert review where the record affects tax, business compliance, or high-value benefits.

## A practical example

Picture a trader who searches for "intraday trading income ITR AY 2026-27" after spotting a portal message, an employer record, a bank credit, or some application requirement. Rather than rushing to file or apply, the better move is to pull the documents together, work through the official source links below, and jot down a short note setting out the facts. The moment a name, bank, Aadhaar, PAN, income, or TDS detail does not line up, stop and sort out the mismatch before leaning on that record.

Yes, this takes longer than copying a one-size-fits-all answer. It is also far less likely to backfire. In practice, most tax and scheme troubles do not come from misreading the main rule. They come from details that quietly disagree with each other across documents.

## Review notes for intraday traders

A reviewer should be able to confirm five things at a glance: the user profile, the official source checked, the documents seen, any unresolved mismatch, and the next action. When the file touches AY 2026-27 ITR filing, note the income head, the ITR form, the tax regime, the TDS or TCS credit, and the e-verification status separately. When it touches a government scheme, record the scheme portal, the application reference, the eligibility documents, and bank-credit readiness on their own line.
