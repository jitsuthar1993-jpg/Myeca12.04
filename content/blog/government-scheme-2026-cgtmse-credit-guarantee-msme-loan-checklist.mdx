# CGTMSE Credit Guarantee Checklist for MSME Loans

CGTMSE is connected to a lender-led credit process; a business should not treat the guarantee framework as a direct cash application. Prepare the loan request, enterprise identity, Udyam details, financial information, and lender correspondence as one consistent credit file.



## Decide the borrowing need before discussing a guarantee

CGTMSE belongs within a lender-led credit process; it is not a direct cash application by the enterprise. The business decision therefore starts with the proposed borrowing. Describe the enterprise, amount required, intended use, timing, existing obligations, expected cash flow, and repayment source. Match the request to the same legal or proprietor identity shown across PAN, Udyam, GST records where applicable, and the operating bank account. The purpose and amount should be understandable without invoking the guarantee framework as the reason the business deserves credit.

Prepare financial information, quotations, orders, or other operating evidence that genuinely supports the request. Do not inflate a project, create unsupported sales, or describe a guarantee as an approval. The lender will assess the credit proposal through its process and may ask questions beyond the documents initially presented. The enterprise should be ready to explain the business facts and respond to those questions consistently.

## Keep enterprise evidence and lender decisions separate

The enterprise owns the accuracy of its business proposal and source registrations. The issuers of PAN, Udyam, GST, bank, and financial records own corrections to those records. The lender controls appraisal, information requests, sanction or rejection, conditions, disbursement, and communication about any guarantee-related treatment within the credit process. A mismatch in Udyam or PAN should be corrected at its source; a disagreement about appraisal belongs with the lender handling the proposal.

Preserve the exact loan request submitted, including the version of financial information and quotations considered. Record every lender query and the response supplied. If the lender discusses a guarantee framework, keep that communication with the credit file but do not present it as money due directly from CGTMSE. Likewise, a complete registration file does not establish viability, repayment capacity, or a lender decision.

## Trace the credit proposal through every lender stage

Build a chronology beginning with the first formal loan approach. Record the branch or channel, submission date, proposal version, acknowledgement, requests for further information, appraisal discussions, decision, conditions, and any disbursement stages. If the application is delayed, ask which lender stage is pending and what specific information remains outstanding. Repeatedly submitting the same bundle without identifying the open question can create conflicting versions.

After a sanction or rejection, preserve the lender's communication and the enterprise's response. For disbursement, connect each release or condition to the approved purpose and actual business record. If a correction is made to enterprise identity or financial information during review, keep the earlier version and explain the change. This trail shows how the lender assessed the request and prevents a guarantee-related reference from being mistaken for a separate approval or direct entitlement.

The enterprise should also record any question it asked specifically about guarantee-related treatment and the lender's answer. That exchange belongs beside the appraisal record, not in promotional material or a separate claim against the framework. It helps later advisers distinguish what the lender considered, what conditions it imposed, and what the borrower merely expected before receiving the formal response.

## CGTMSE Credit Guarantee: source pages and next actions

Read [CGTMSE official portal](https://www.cgtmse.in/) for the current instruction affecting review of guarantee-linked loan records, lender process, and business documentation. Keep that CGTMSE Credit Guarantee page and its check date with the application record, and route an error in the underlying source to the issuer or programme channel that owns the disputed fact.
