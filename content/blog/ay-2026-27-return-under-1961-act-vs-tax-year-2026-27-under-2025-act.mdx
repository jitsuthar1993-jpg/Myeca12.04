# AY 2026-27 Return Under 1961 Act vs Tax Year 2026-27 Under 2025 Act

A transition guide explaining why AY 2026-27 ITR filing and Tax Year 2026-27 compliance can sit under different practical timelines during the new income-tax law rollout.

This guide is written for Indian taxpayers, founders, finance teams, and return filers adjusting to the Income-tax Act, 2025 and Finance Act 2025 changes. It is an educational readiness note, not a promise of tax outcome, refund speed, or notice avoidance. Always match the position with the official portal, notified forms, and the taxpayer's own documents.

## The core distinction

India's tax calendar now carries two co-existing reference points. The term "assessment year" belongs to the Income-tax Act, 1961 framework, while "tax year" is the new concept introduced by the Income-tax Act, 2025. They are not interchangeable, and using the wrong one when labelling a return, a payment, or a TDS filing can create reconciliation headaches.

| Point | Practical meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | AY 2026-27 relates to income earned in FY 2025-26. |
| 2 | Tax Year 2026-27 starts on 1 April 2026 under the new Act framework. |
| 3 | Mixing both periods can cause wrong-form, wrong-payment, and wrong-notice assumptions. |

## Why the overlap creates problems

Until the Income-tax Act, 2025 is fully in force for all taxpayer categories, the two frameworks run in parallel. Your Form 16 for the salary paid in FY 2025-26 belongs to AY 2026-27 under the 1961 Act. Your employer's April 2026 TDS deposit, on the other hand, belongs to Tax Year 2026-27 under the new framework.

Filing the AY 2026-27 return requires the old framework's forms, schedules, and section references. Responding to a TDS demand for April 2026 onwards requires the new framework. Confusing the two — for instance, using a new-Act form for an old-period return — can cause the portal to reject the filing or, worse, create a mismatch that the department picks up in processing.

## Records to keep separately

- FY 2025-26 salary and TDS records (for the AY 2026-27 return)
- April 2026 onwards payroll records (for Tax Year 2026-27 compliance)
- Portal confirmation of which ITR utility was used
- A brief working note that tags each document to the correct period and law

Keeping the two sets of records in separate folders is not administrative fussiness — it is the fastest way to respond if a notice arrives six months later asking about a specific period.

## Step-by-step checklist

- Identify whether your immediate task is AY 2026-27 filing, Tax Year 2026-27 compliance, or both.
- Read the official source and map the rule to your income head, taxpayer type, and dates.
- Collect source records, computation notes, challans, statements, and declarations before filing or payment.
- Check whether the position changes the ITR form, schedule, tax payment, TDS/TCS, or disclosure route.
- Preserve the final return, acknowledgement, e-verification proof, and supporting working papers.

## Common errors to avoid

- Using a familiar old form number without checking the current official form on the portal.
- Mixing AY 2026-27 filing records with Tax Year 2026-27 payment or TDS records.
- Treating a headline slab, rebate, or threshold announcement as a final computation without checking the notified rules.
- Filing or paying before reconciling AIS, Form 26AS, challans, books, and certificates.
- Not preserving the official source and computation note used for the decision.
