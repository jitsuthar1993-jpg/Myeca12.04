# AY 2026-27 Crypto VDA Tax Records Checklist

If you traded or transferred crypto and other virtual digital assets during FY 2025-26, the hard part of AY 2026-27 filing is rarely the headline rule. It is paperwork. Exchange reports, VDA transfer records, TDS data, and your bank trail all have to survive until you actually sit down to file. This guide walks through that, and it is deliberately conservative. There is no refund promise here, no processing-time guarantee, and nothing that replaces a look at the official filing utility.

## Who tends to land on this page

In our experience, the people searching this topic are crypto and VDA users who already recognise the broad label but are stuck on the specifics: which documents to keep, which portal is authoritative, how to reconcile tax records, and what to do next. The mistake we see most often is lifting a stray answer off the internet and pasting it straight into a return or application. Better to begin with official sources and then assemble a document file that someone can actually review.

For MyeCA readers, that boils down to three habits. Keep the assessment-year or scheme-year context unambiguous. Reconcile names, PAN, Aadhaar, bank account, and income figures before you hit submit. And hold on to the acknowledgement or application reference, because chasing a later refund, subsidy, loan, or notice is far harder once that number is lost.

## The checklist at a glance

- Verify the official portal or Income Tax Department source before you act on anything.
- Park identity, bank, income, and scheme-specific documents together in a single folder.
- Reconcile AIS, Form 26AS, Form 16, Form 16A, GST, or bank records wherever they are relevant.
- Never treat eligibility, a refund, a subsidy, or a loan approval as settled without official verification.
- Bring in a CA or expert when the record touches tax, business compliance, or a high-value benefit.

## How this plays out in practice

Picture a user who types "crypto VDA tax records AY 2026-27" into a search box after a portal message, an employer record, a bank credit, or some application requirement nudges them. Rather than file or apply on the spot, they gather the documents, check the official links listed below, and jot down a short note setting out the facts. The moment a name, bank, Aadhaar, PAN, income, or TDS detail fails to line up, they stop and resolve the mismatch before leaning on that record.

Yes, this is slower than copy-pasting a generic answer. It is also a lot safer. A surprising share of tax and scheme trouble has nothing to do with the main rule; it comes from small details that disagree with each other across documents.

## Review notes for crypto and VDA users

A reviewer should pin down the user profile, the official source checked, the documents seen, any unresolved mismatch, and the next action. Where the file feeds AY 2026-27 ITR filing, the note should also call out the income head, the ITR form, the tax regime, the TDS or TCS credit, and the e-verification status. Where it feeds a government scheme instead, the note should record the scheme portal, the application reference, the eligibility documents, and whether bank-credit readiness is in place.
