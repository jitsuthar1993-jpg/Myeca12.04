# Self-Assessment Tax Challan Under the 2025 Act: Payment Guide

A taxpayer guide to self-assessment tax payment under the Income-tax Act, 2025 transition, including challans, year selection, and return matching.

This guide is for Indian taxpayers, founders, and finance teams navigating AY 2026-27 filing alongside the Income-tax Act, 2025 and Finance Act 2025 changes. It is an educational readiness note — not a promise of tax outcome, refund speed, or notice avoidance. Cross-check every position against the official portal, notified forms, and your own source documents.

## What changed

Tax payment records are an integral part of the return, not an afterthought. The challan must show the right assessment or tax-year context, the correct amount, the right tax type, and the taxpayer's PAN. A mismatch between the challan and the return schedule is enough to generate a demand notice that could have been avoided entirely.

| Point | Practical meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Payment should be made under the correct year and tax type. |
| 2 | Challan details must match the return computation. |
| 3 | A wrong challan should be resolved before relying on credit. |

## Why it matters now

The transition to the Income-tax Act, 2025 creates a year-selection problem that trips up even careful filers. AY 2026-27 return work, Tax Year 2026-27 current compliance, outstanding notices, and newly notified forms can land on your desk in the same month. Keeping a clean file means recording the period clearly, noting the applicable law reference, confirming the portal form type, specifying whether it is a payment or a return, and attaching supporting evidence for each position you take.

## Practical example

Before hitting the submit button on an AY 2026-27 return, compare the challan CIN, date, amount, and tax type against the return's tax-paid schedule. If these do not agree, the system will flag a demand. A ten-minute reconciliation at this stage is far cheaper than a rectification or grievance later.

## Records to keep

- Challan receipt
- Computation worksheet
- Return tax-paid schedule
- Bank debit proof

## Step-by-step checklist

- Identify whether self-assessment tax payment affects AY 2026-27 filing, Tax Year 2026-27 compliance, or both.
- Read the official source and map the rule to your income head, taxpayer type, and dates.
- Collect source records, computation notes, challans, statements, and declarations before filing or payment.
- Check whether the position changes the ITR form, schedule, tax payment, TDS/TCS, or disclosure route.
- Preserve the final return, acknowledgement, e-verification proof, and supporting working papers.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Using a familiar old form number without checking the current official form.
- Mixing AY 2026-27 filing records with Tax Year 2026-27 payment or TDS records.
- Treating a headline slab, rebate, or threshold as a final computation figure without actually running the numbers.
- Filing or paying before reconciling AIS, Form 26AS, challans, books, and certificates against each other.
- Not preserving the official source and a brief computation note alongside the filing decision.
