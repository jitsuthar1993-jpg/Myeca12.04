# Atal Pension Yojana Eligibility and Bank Mandate Checklist

Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is a government-backed pension scheme targeted at workers in the unorganised sector. Enrollment happens through your savings bank account, and contributions are auto-debited monthly. The scheme guarantees a fixed pension at age 60, with the pension amount depending on how much you contribute and at what age you join. For workers who do not have access to employer-sponsored provident funds or gratuity, APY is often the most accessible pension product available.

This checklist covers what you need to check before enrolling — not what the scheme promises in every circumstance.

## Eligibility basics to verify first

APY is open to Indian citizens between the ages of 18 and 40. You must have a savings bank account, and you must not already be a beneficiary of any statutory social security scheme. If you have an existing NPS (National Pension System) account, your bank may ask for clarification before setting up APY, since the two schemes are administered through the same CRA infrastructure.

Verify your eligibility directly with your bank or on the NPS CRA portal before filling out any form. Bank branch staff are generally trained to handle APY enrollment, and the process is simpler than most people expect.

## The bank mandate: the detail that matters most

APY contributions are collected through an auto-debit mandate on your bank account. The mandate must have sufficient balance on the contribution date — if the account runs short, the bank charges a penalty and the contribution for that period is missed. Repeated failures can lead to the account being frozen under the scheme rules.

Before enrolling, decide on the contribution amount you can sustain comfortably throughout the year and confirm that your salary or income reliably reaches your account a few days before the auto-debit date. A mismatch between your income cycle and your APY debit date is the most common reason for contribution penalties.

## Nominee registration: do not skip this

APY requires you to register a nominee. In the event of death before age 60, the nominee receives the accumulated pension wealth. If you die after 60, the spouse receives the pension, and upon the spouse's death the nominee receives the pension corpus.

This is not optional or cosmetic — it directly affects who receives the benefit. Make sure the nominee's details (name, date of birth, relationship) are filled in accurately and kept updated if your family situation changes.

## Tax treatment of APY contributions

Contributions to APY qualify for deduction under Section 80CCD(1) of the Income Tax Act 1961, within the overall ₹1.5 lakh ceiling of Section 80C. If you are filing an ITR for AY 2026-27, ensure the contribution amount is reflected correctly. Your bank will typically issue a statement showing annual APY contributions, which you can use to support the deduction claim.

If you are salaried and your employer does not include APY in Form 16, claim the deduction yourself in your ITR by entering the contribution amount in the relevant schedule.

## For the reviewer

Check that the APY contribution deduction is being claimed under Section 80CCD(1) and that it does not exceed the permissible limit when combined with other 80C investments. If the subscriber is also contributing to NPS through an employer, the additional deduction under Section 80CCD(1B) (up to ₹50,000) may apply separately — confirm the ITR reflects this correctly. Note the scheme portal used, the PRAN or subscriber reference, and whether any penalty amounts appear in the bank statement for missed contributions.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Atal Pension Yojana eligibility guaranteed by this guide?

No. Eligibility depends on the official portal, current scheme rules, state or ministry verification, and the applicant's documents.

### Should I use only social media information before applying?

No. Use social posts only to identify the issue, then verify the rule and application status on official government sources.

### Why keep tax records for a government scheme?

Many applications ask for income, bank, identity, or business records. A clean document trail reduces avoidable mismatch and follow-up questions.

## The bottom line

APY enrollment takes about fifteen minutes at your bank branch. The preparation — confirming eligibility, ensuring your bank balance rhythm works for auto-debit, registering a nominee, and noting the tax deduction — takes maybe another thirty. That time is well spent.
