# Atal Pension Yojana Eligibility and Bank Mandate Checklist

Before enrolment, trace the subscriber's age, bank mandate, contribution instruction, and nominee details to the same person. A successful first debit does not prove that every later contribution or nominee field is correct, so preserve the enrolment record and review account entries.

Treat the pension choice and the bank mandate as separate records. The subscriber file should show the selected pension option, age and identity details used at enrolment, contribution instruction, debit history, nominee information, and every acknowledged correction. Investigate failed or unexpected debits through the bank or official channel. Compare the contribution instruction with the actual debit pattern and keep a dated explanation for a missed, delayed, or changed contribution. Do not use a successful debit alone as proof that nominee, pension option, or later account details are correct.

## Make the pension and debit choices consciously

Before enrolment, the subscriber should understand that the pension selection and the bank debit instruction are related but separate decisions. Confirm the subscriber's identity, age entry, selected pension option, contribution instruction, operating bank account, and nominee details as they will appear at enrolment. Review the current official terms through the bank or authorised channel rather than relying on an old contribution table or another subscriber's experience. The account must be usable for the mandate, but an active account alone does not confirm that every enrolment field is right.

Read the proposed debit instruction in plain language with the subscriber. Note the expected contribution, timing, and account from which it will be collected. If the person is uncertain about the option or cannot maintain the intended account, resolve that before accepting the mandate. Do not treat the first successful debit as evidence that the pension choice, nominee, or future contribution sequence has been fully verified.

## Keep bank mandates and subscriber details under the right owners

The subscriber owns the choices and personal information supplied. The bank or authorised enrolment channel controls the mandate setup and its acknowledgement, while the pension record controls the option, contribution history, and recognised updates shown through the programme route. A nominee correction, bank-account problem, and identity mismatch are different matters. Each should be raised through the channel that can change that record.

Preserve the enrolment form or acknowledgement, mandate details, nominee entry, and the bank statement showing actual debits. When an unexpected or failed debit appears, first identify the period and compare it with the instruction on record. Ask the bank or official channel for the reason and retain the response. Avoid replacing the original enrolment record with a corrected copy; keeping both versions shows whether later contributions were affected by the change.

## Reconcile every contribution event over time

Follow-up should begin after enrolment, not only when a problem becomes serious. Record the first debit, each later expected debit, any missed or reversed transaction, and every correction request. For a failed debit, note when it was noticed, the account position, the channel contacted, the explanation received, and the next contribution outcome. This timeline helps distinguish a mandate problem from an account issue or a subscriber-detail correction.

Review nominee and contact details periodically through the recognised route and preserve acknowledgements for changes. If the bank account changes, document the old account's final contribution position and the effective date of the new instruction. A reliable file should let the subscriber or nominee trace the pension option, mandate, contribution history, and corrections without inferring current status from a single debit entry.

When a contribution pattern differs from what the subscriber expected, calculate the periods and amounts in question before escalating. Ask for an explanation tied to those entries, not a broad assurance that the account is active. Preserve any instruction about making good a missed contribution or changing the mandate exactly as received, and confirm the later account result before treating the matter as closed.
