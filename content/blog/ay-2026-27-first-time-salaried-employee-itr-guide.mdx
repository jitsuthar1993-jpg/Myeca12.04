# AY 2026-27 ITR Guide for First-Time Salaried Employees

Your first salary slip eventually turns into your first tax return, and that is where most new earners feel stuck. The questions are rarely about the law itself. They are about getting the assessment year right, reading Form 16 correctly, remembering to declare bank interest, and finishing e-verification without panic. This guide is written for FY 2025-26 income that you will report in AY 2026-27. Treat it as a careful filing checklist. It does not promise a refund, a fixed processing time, or anything that replaces checking the official utility yourself.

## Who this guide is really for

If you have searched for this topic, you probably already know the broad idea of filing an ITR. What you are unsure about is the detail: which documents matter, which portal is official, how your tax records line up, and what the next step actually is. The biggest mistake here is lifting a random answer off the internet and dropping it straight into your return or an application. A better habit is to start from official sources and slowly build a document file that someone can review later.

For MyeCA readers, three things keep this simple. Be clear about the assessment-year or scheme-year you are dealing with. Make sure your name, PAN, Aadhaar, bank account, and income figures agree across every record before you submit anything. And hold on to the acknowledgement or application reference, because any later refund, subsidy, loan, or notice gets much harder to handle once that number is lost.

## Quick checklist

- Confirm the official portal or Income Tax Department source before acting.
- Keep identity, bank, income, and scheme-specific documents in one folder.
- Match AIS, Form 26AS, Form 16, Form 16A, GST, or bank records where they apply.
- Do not assume eligibility, refund, subsidy, or loan approval without official verification.
- Use a CA or expert review where the record affects tax, business compliance, or high-value benefits.

## A worked example

Picture someone who looks up "first time salaried employee ITR AY 2026-27" after spotting a portal message, an employer record, a bank credit, or some application requirement. Rather than filing or applying on the spot, they gather their documents, open the official source links given below, and jot down a short note setting out the facts as they stand. The moment a name, bank, Aadhaar, PAN, income, or TDS mismatch surfaces, they stop and correct it before trusting that record any further.

Yes, this is slower than pasting in a generic answer. It is also far safer. In practice, most tax and scheme trouble does not come from the headline rule. It comes from small details that do not agree with each other across documents.

## What a reviewer should note

When a reviewer goes through a first-time filer's case, the note should capture the user profile, the official source that was checked, the documents seen, any mismatch left unresolved, and the next action. For an AY 2026-27 ITR filing, the note should also record the income head, the ITR form, the tax regime, the TDS or TCS credit, and the e-verification status. Where a government scheme is involved instead, it should separately cover the scheme portal, the application reference, the eligibility documents, and whether the bank account is ready to receive a credit.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this article a substitute for professional advice?

No. Use it as an educational checklist and get case-specific review where documents, income heads, or eligibility are unclear.

### Which year does this AY 2026-27 guide cover?

AY 2026-27 generally relates to FY 2025-26 income, subject to the facts of the taxpayer and official filing utility rules.

### What should I check before filing?

Check the ITR form, tax regime, AIS, Form 26AS, TDS certificates, bank details, and the documents supporting the income or deduction.
