# AY 2026-27 Education Loan Interest Deduction Guide

If you are repaying an education loan, the questions usually start at filing time: which papers prove the interest you paid, whether the borrower relationship qualifies, and how to claim the deduction under the old regime without tripping over a mismatch. This guide walks through that, treating FY 2025-26 income being filed for AY 2026-27. Think of it as a careful checklist for getting the file in order, not a promise about refund amounts or processing speed, and certainly not a replacement for checking the live filing utility.

## Who this is for

People who land on this topic typically already recognise the heading but feel shaky on the details, the documents, which government portal to trust, the tax records to match, and what to actually do next. The one habit worth keeping is to never lift a stray answer off the internet and drop it straight into a return or application. Begin at the official sources, then assemble a document file that someone can sit down and review.

For MyeCA readers that comes down to three habits. Keep the assessment year or scheme year you are working in clearly in view. Reconcile your name, PAN, Aadhaar, bank account, and income figures before anything is submitted. And hold on to every acknowledgement or application reference, because chasing a refund, subsidy, loan, or notice later is far harder once that number goes missing.

## Quick checklist

- Confirm the official portal or Income Tax Department source before acting.
- Keep identity, bank, income, and scheme-specific documents in one folder.
- Match AIS, Form 26AS, Form 16, Form 16A, GST, or bank records where they apply.
- Do not assume eligibility, refund, subsidy, or loan approval without official verification.
- Use a CA or expert review where the record affects tax, business compliance, or high-value benefits.

## A worked example

Picture someone who looks up "education loan interest deduction AY 2026-27" after spotting a portal message, an employer entry, a bank credit, or a requirement on some application. The better move is not to file or apply on the spot. Instead, they gather the documents, open the official source links given below, and jot down a short note setting out the facts. The moment a name, bank, Aadhaar, PAN, income, or TDS figure refuses to line up, they stop and sort out that mismatch before leaning on the record.

Yes, this takes longer than copying a one-size-fits-all answer. It is also the safer route. A surprising share of tax and scheme trouble has nothing to do with the headline rule. It comes from small details that disagree with each other across documents.
