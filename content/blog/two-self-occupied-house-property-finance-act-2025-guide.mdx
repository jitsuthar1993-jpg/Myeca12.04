# Two Self-Occupied House Properties: Finance Act 2025 Guide

Understand the Finance Act 2025 update for two self-occupied house properties and how homeowners should prepare records for AY 2026-27 filing.

This guide is intended for Indian taxpayers, founders, and finance teams working through the changes introduced by the Income-tax Act, 2025 and Finance Act 2025. It is an educational readiness note — not a guarantee of any particular tax outcome or a substitute for professional filing advice. Always verify the position against the official portal, notified forms, and the actual documents in front of you.

## Understanding what the change means in practice

House-property reporting is not just a count of properties. Occupancy status, rental income, loan interest, co-ownership, and ITR form eligibility each play a role.

| Point | Practical meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Homeowners should identify self-occupied and let-out treatment carefully. |
| 2 | Loan interest records should be matched with property status. |
| 3 | Form selection can change when house-property facts become complex. |

## The year-selection problem this transition creates

AY 2026-27 return work, Tax Year 2026-27 ongoing compliance, outstanding notices from earlier years, and new forms under the revised law can all land on a taxpayer's desk in the same month. It is easy to mix things up. A clean file should clearly show the period covered, the applicable law reference, the portal form used, the payment or return type, and the supporting evidence — all in one place.

## Worked example

A taxpayer with two homes and one rented flat should prepare a property-by-property table before deciding whether ITR-1 is appropriate or whether a different form is required. Rent receipts, loan certificates, and municipal tax records for each property should be assembled separately so the interest and income figures can be matched cleanly.

## Records every homeowner should collect

- Property-wise ownership details (including co-owner shares where applicable)
- Loan interest certificates
- Rent records and tenancy agreements
- Municipal tax receipts

## How to work through the filing

- Determine whether house-property review affects AY 2026-27 filing, Tax Year 2026-27 compliance, or both.
- Check the official source and map the applicable rule to your income head, taxpayer type, and relevant dates.
- Collect source records, computation workings, challans, statements, and declarations before filing or making payment.
- Verify whether the position changes the ITR form, any schedule, tax payment, TDS/TCS treatment, or disclosure requirement.
- Preserve the final return, e-verification proof, and supporting working papers once done.

## Where filings go wrong

- Using a familiar old form number without checking the current official form for the assessment year.
- Mixing AY 2026-27 filing records with Tax Year 2026-27 payment or TDS records.
- Reading a headline slab rate, rebate, or threshold as the final computation without running the numbers.
- Filing or making payment before reconciling AIS, Form 26AS, challans, books, and certificates.
- Not keeping the official source and the computation note that supported the filing decision.

No. It is an educational readiness note. Use the official portal and get case-specific CA review where facts, amounts, residency, or notices are complex.

### Should I rely only on prefilled data?

No. Prefill is useful, but the return position should be matched with Form 16, AIS, Form 26AS, bank records, books, broker reports, challans, and working papers.
