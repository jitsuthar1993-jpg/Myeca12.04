# AICTE Pragati and Saksham Scholarship Readiness Guide

A scholarship file should show the student's current course, recognised institution, admission status, family-income period, and bank holder without contradictions. Institution verification can be as important as the student's upload, so retain the submitted academic details and any verification response.


Keep the scholarship notice and applicant category beside the student file. Do not merge two scholarship paths simply because they share an application channel or ask for similar academic records. The working should identify the course, institution, admission stage, category-specific evidence, family-income period, and bank holder used for this application. If the institution changes a course or verification detail, preserve the earlier submission and the institution response so the correction can be traced to the right academic cycle.

## Choose the scholarship path from the current student record

The first applicant decision is to identify the specific scholarship path being considered and test the student's current academic facts against its official notice. Similar application channels do not make Pragati and Saksham interchangeable. Record the course, institution, admission status, academic year, and the category-specific fact relevant to the chosen path. Use the notice for that cycle as the reference point, and avoid filling a form from memory or from another student's experience.

Before submission, reconcile the student's name, course title, institution details, admission record, family-income period, and bank-account holder. A mismatch does not automatically mean the student is ineligible; it means the responsible source must be checked before the form is relied on. Preserve the academic and category evidence actually used. Do not create a category claim, alter a course description, or assume recognition merely because an institution appears in another portal or earlier cycle.

## Separate student entries from institution verification

The student owns the accuracy of personal details and uploaded evidence. The institution controls its enrolment, course, and verification records, while the income-certificate issuer and bank each control their own documents. The scholarship authority controls the programme decision and status shown after those stages. When a field is questioned, direct the correction to the organisation that can authoritatively change it rather than uploading a different document that answers another question.

Keep a copy of the submitted form before institution verification. If the institution identifies an error in course, admission, or academic details, retain its message and the corrected submission together. A bank correction should be supported by the student's account record and should not overwrite academic evidence. Likewise, a revised income certificate should preserve the issuing authority, covered period, and relationship to the same application cycle. These boundaries make later payment or verification questions understandable.

## Track one academic cycle from submission to payment

Build the chronology around the relevant academic year. Record the application identifier, submission date, institution-verification stage, correction message, revised submission, programme status, and any payment communication. If a status remains unchanged, ask the owner of the current stage rather than sending the same query to every organisation. Institution verification, programme selection, and bank payment are separate events and may require different references.

For renewal or continuation, open a new cycle record while carrying forward only facts that remain current. Note changes in course year, institution status, family-income evidence, contact details, or bank account, and preserve the prior cycle instead of replacing it. A complete file should show what the student submitted, what the institution verified, which correction was made, and what happened afterward without implying an approval that the official status does not show.

If two possible scholarship paths remain plausible, preserve the notice and comparison that led to the final choice. Record why the student's facts were presented under one path and not the other, then keep any authority clarification received. That decision note is especially useful when similar portal labels or institution advice later create doubt about the category originally selected.

## AICTE Pragati and Saksham Scholarship: source pages and next actions

Read [AICTE official portal](https://www.aicte-india.org/) for the current instruction affecting institute recognition, student records, family income proof, and portal deadlines. Keep that AICTE Pragati and Saksham Scholarship page and its check date with the application record, and route an error in the underlying source to the issuer or programme channel that owns the disputed fact.
