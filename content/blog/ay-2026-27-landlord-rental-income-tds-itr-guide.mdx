# AY 2026-27 Landlord Rental Income and TDS ITR Guide

If you let out property, the hard part of filing is rarely the rule itself. It is getting five documents to agree with each other: the rent agreement, the municipal tax you paid, the interest on your home loan, any TDS your tenant deducted, and what finally shows up in Form 26AS. This guide walks landlords earning rent through that reconciliation for FY 2025-26 income, which is reported in AY 2026-27. Treat it as a careful filing checklist. It does not promise a refund, predict a processing time, or replace a look at the live official utility.

## Who lands on this guide

Most people who reach this page already know they have rental income to report. What trips them up is the detail work: which documents to gather, which portal is authoritative, how their tax records line up, and what to actually do next. The single worst move is to lift a generic answer off the internet and drop it straight into a return or an application. Begin with the official sources, then assemble a document file that someone can review later.

For MyeCA readers, that boils down to three habits. Keep the assessment-year or scheme-year context straight so you do not mix periods. Make sure your name, PAN, Aadhaar, bank account, and income records all match before you submit anything. And hold on to the acknowledgement or application reference, because refund, subsidy, loan, or notice work down the line gets much harder without it.

## Quick checklist

- Confirm the official portal or Income Tax Department source before acting.
- Keep identity, bank, income, and scheme-specific documents in one folder.
- Match AIS, Form 26AS, Form 16, Form 16A, GST, or bank records where they apply.
- Do not assume eligibility, refund, subsidy, or loan approval without official verification.
- Use a CA or expert review where the record affects tax, business compliance, or high-value benefits.

## A worked example

Take a landlord who searches "rental income TDS ITR AY 2026-27" after spotting a portal message, an employer record, a bank credit, or some application requirement. Rather than rushing to file or apply, they pull the documents together, open the official source links listed below, and jot down a short note setting out the facts. The moment a name, bank, Aadhaar, PAN, income, or TDS figure does not match across those records, they stop and resolve the mismatch first instead of building on top of it.

Yes, this is slower than pasting in a ready-made answer. It is also a lot safer. In practice, most tax and scheme problems are not failures of the main rule at all. They come from small details that disagree from one document to the next.

## Review notes for landlords earning rent

When the file goes for review, the reviewer should pin down the user profile, the official source consulted, the documents actually seen, any unresolved mismatch, and the next action. Where the file feeds into AY 2026-27 ITR filing, the note should call out the income head, the ITR form, the tax regime, the TDS or TCS credit, and the e-verification status separately. Where it relates to a government scheme, it should separately record the scheme portal, the application reference, the eligibility documents, and whether the bank account is ready for any credit.
