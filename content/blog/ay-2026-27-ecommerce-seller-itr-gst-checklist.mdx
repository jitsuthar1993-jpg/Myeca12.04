# AY 2026-27 Ecommerce Seller ITR and GST Checklist

If you sell on marketplaces, the hardest part of filing is rarely the tax rule itself. It is getting four different sets of numbers to agree: your marketplace settlement statements, your GST returns, the TDS or TCS that has been reported against you, and the turnover you finally declare in your income-tax return. This guide walks ecommerce sellers through that reconciliation for FY 2025-26 income being filed for AY 2026-27. Treat it as a cautious, document-first checklist, not a promise about refunds, processing times, or a replacement for what the official filing utility actually shows.

## Who this is for

The people landing on this topic usually already know the headline. They sell online, they know a return is due, but they are unsure which documents matter, which portal to trust, or what to do next. The trap is copying a quick answer off the internet straight into a return or application. A steadier route is to begin with official sources and then assemble a file that a reviewer can actually check.

For MyeCA readers that comes down to three habits. Keep the assessment year and scheme year clearly in view so you are working with the right period. Reconcile your name, PAN, Aadhaar, bank account, and income records against each other before anything is submitted. And hold on to every acknowledgement or application reference, because any later refund, subsidy, loan, or notice becomes much harder to handle once that number is lost.

## Quick checklist

- Confirm the official portal or Income Tax Department source before acting.
- Keep identity, bank, income, and scheme-specific documents in one folder.
- Match AIS, Form 26AS, Form 16, Form 16A, GST, or bank records where they apply.
- Do not assume eligibility, refund, subsidy, or loan approval without official verification.
- Use a CA or expert review where the record affects tax, business compliance, or high-value benefits.

## A practical example

Picture a seller who searches "ecommerce seller ITR GST AY 2026-27" after spotting a portal message, an employer record, a bank credit, or some application requirement. Rather than rushing to file or apply, the better move is to gather the documents first, open the official source links below, and jot down a short note setting out the facts. The moment a name, bank, Aadhaar, PAN, income, or TDS mismatch turns up, the seller stops and resolves it before treating the record as final.

Yes, this is slower than pasting in a generic answer. It is also far safer. In practice, most tax and scheme headaches do not come from misreading the main rule. They come from small details that do not line up across documents.

## Review notes for ecommerce sellers

A reviewer should be able to read off the seller profile, the official source that was checked, the documents that were seen, any unresolved mismatch, and the next action. Where the file touches AY 2026-27 ITR filing, the note should also record the income head, the ITR form, the tax regime, the TDS or TCS credit, and the e-verification status. Where a government scheme is involved, it should separately capture the scheme portal, the application reference, the eligibility documents, and whether the bank account is ready for credit.
