# Trade License Registration for Shops, Restaurants, and Local Businesses

Trade license work is practical and local. A shop, office, restaurant, warehouse, clinic, salon, coaching center, manufacturing unit, or service outlet may need permission from a local authority depending on the city, business activity, premises, and applicable municipal rules.

Because rules vary by location, the safest approach is not to copy a generic checklist. Start by identifying the local authority and business activity.

## Map the business activity

Write down the exact activity: retail, food service, storage, repair, professional office, manufacturing, distribution, ecommerce support, or another activity. The activity may decide which local permissions or supporting documents are required.

For food businesses, trade license work should be coordinated with FSSAI registration or license review. For factories or sensitive activities, other clearances may also matter.

## Premises proof matters

Local authorities usually want to understand where the business operates. Keep rent agreement, ownership proof, electricity bill, property tax record where available, NOC from owner, layout or area details, and address photographs if requested.

If the premises use is not consistent with the business activity, resolve that before submission.

## Documents to prepare

| File area | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Applicant KYC | PAN, Aadhaar, photo, contact details |
| Entity proof | Incorporation certificate, partnership deed, LLP agreement, GST or Udyam |
| Premises proof | Rent agreement, owner NOC, electricity bill, property proof |
| Activity proof | Food license, shop details, product or service description, local forms |
| Renewals | Previous license, fee receipt, inspection or query communication |

Before applying, confirm city, ward or authority, activity, premises documents, owner consent, related licenses, and renewal obligations. After approval, save the certificate and next renewal date.
