# Capital Loss Carry Forward Under the New Income-tax Law

A practical guide to preserving short-term and long-term capital-loss records and using them correctly during the Income-tax Act, 2025 transition.

This guide is for Indian taxpayers, founders, finance teams, and return filers working through the changes brought by the Income-tax Act, 2025 and Finance Act 2025. Treat it as an educational readiness note — not a guarantee of any tax outcome, refund processing speed, or protection from notices. Before filing, verify every position against the official portal, notified forms, and your own records.

## What changed

A capital loss cannot be treated as a single generic number carried from one year to the next. Asset category, holding period, special-rate transactions, and the specific disclosure schedule all affect how the loss is recorded and how much of it can be used.

| Point | Practical meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Capital-loss records should identify asset type, period, and return year. |
| 2 | Broker reports should be reconciled with AIS and the return schedule. |
| 3 | Carry-forward is easier to defend when old acknowledgements are preserved. |

## Why it matters now

The shift to the Income-tax Act, 2025 has produced a year-selection problem that can catch taxpayers off guard. AY 2026-27 return work, Tax Year 2026-27 current compliance, old pending notices, and newly notified forms can all surface together in the same filing period. A clean loss carry-forward file needs to be very specific: it should state the period the loss belongs to, the applicable law reference, the portal form used, and the supporting evidence — whether that is a broker statement, an AIS entry, or an older ITR acknowledgement.

## Practical example

A taxpayer holding listed shares, equity mutual funds, and crypto or VDA instruments may have losses across all three categories. Short-term and long-term capital losses from each category carry different set-off and carry-forward treatment under the Income Tax Act, 1961. Before claiming any carry-forward, separate each category, match the broker-reported figures against AIS, and verify that the earlier return in which the loss was first claimed has been filed within the due date. A carry-forward that was not properly disclosed in the original return may not be available to set off in a later year.

## Records to keep

- Broker capital-gains statement showing transaction-level detail for each asset category
- AIS entries for the corresponding securities and VDA transactions
- Earlier ITR acknowledgements for every year the loss was first reported
- Loss carry-forward schedule from those earlier returns

## Step-by-step checklist

- Identify whether capital loss carry-forward review affects AY 2026-27 filing, Tax Year 2026-27 compliance, or both.
- Read the official source and map the rule to your income head, taxpayer type, and dates.
- Collect source records, computation notes, challans, statements, and declarations before filing or payment.
- Check whether the position changes the ITR form, schedule, tax payment, TDS/TCS, or disclosure route.
- Preserve the final return, acknowledgement, e-verification proof, and supporting working papers.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Reaching for a familiar old form number without first checking what the current notified form requires.
- Mixing AY 2026-27 filing records with Tax Year 2026-27 payment or TDS records — they belong to separate compliance layers.
- Using a headline slab rate or rebate figure as a shortcut to the final tax number without running the actual computation.
- Submitting the return before reconciling AIS, Form 26AS, challans, books, and certificates against each other.
- Losing the official source reference and computation note that justified the carry-forward position in the first place.

## Final checklist

Confirm the assessment year. Read the official rule from an authoritative source. Gather all supporting records. Draft a short computation note showing how the loss arose, which return it was first reported in, and how much remains to carry forward. Verify whether the position changes the ITR form, schedule, or payment obligation. After submission, preserve the acknowledgement and e-verification proof in the same folder as the underlying documents.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this capital loss carry-forward guidance a substitute for filing advice?

No. It is an educational readiness note. Use the official portal and get case-specific CA review where facts, amounts, residency, or notices are complex.

### Should I rely only on prefilled data?

No. Prefill is useful, but the return position should be matched with Form 16, AIS, Form 26AS, bank records, books, broker reports, challans, and working papers.

### What should I preserve after taking a position?

Keep the official reference, computation note, supporting documents, portal acknowledgement, challans, and any professional review note in one folder.
