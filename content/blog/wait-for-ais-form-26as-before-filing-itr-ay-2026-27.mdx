# Should I Wait for Form 26AS and AIS to Update Before Filing ITR?

Yes, in most refund or multi-income cases, wait until AIS, TIS, and Form 26AS reflect the correct TDS and income data. Filing too early can cause mismatch, refund delay, or notice risk.

A practical AY 2026-27 answer to whether you should wait for AIS, TIS, and Form 26AS before filing ITR or claiming refund.

## Form 26AS: Facts that determine the AIS answer

| Point | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | AIS is broader than Form 26AS. |
| 2 | Refund claims should match tax-credit records. |
| 3 | Wrong AIS entries should be reviewed, not ignored. |

The first control is simple: Download AIS, TIS, and Form 26AS. The next control is: Match salary, bank interest, dividend, securities, and TDS. Document any difference involving Form 26AS or TIS.

## Form 26AS: Rule to confirm before filing

The return should disclose all taxable income and claim only valid tax credits. AIS/TIS and Form 26AS are key reconciliation records used before filing.

## Form 26AS: A practical Form 26AS scenario

If your employer TDS is visible but bank FD TDS is not yet visible, a refund claim may be short-supported until the bank's TDS return is processed.

## Form 26AS: Match the issue to the available route

| Situation | Practical next action |
| --- | --- |
| Return not filed yet | Download AIS, TIS, and Form 26AS. Match salary, bank interest, dividend, securities, and TDS. Choose the AY 2026-27 form and schedules that can report AIS. |
| Portal data and personal records differ | Submit AIS feedback for incorrect items. For AIS, explain the difference, submit relevant AIS feedback, and retain the reconciliation note. |
| Return already filed with a mistake | Assess whether revised return, rectification, ITR-U, grievance, or notice response can correct the Form 26AS issue described in the records. |
| Material uncertainty remains | Obtain document-based review before taking a final position on the unresolved Form 26AS issue. |

## Form 26AS: Risks to resolve before submission

- Using only Form 16.
- Ignoring AIS because tax was deducted.
- Claiming refund with missing TDS credit.
- Forgetting small savings interest.

Using only Form 16 and ignoring AIS because tax was deducted can change tax, refund, disclosure, or the evidence available for a later response; resolve both before submission.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is AIS always correct?

No. AIS can contain incorrect or duplicate information, so review and submit feedback where needed.

### Is Form 26AS still important?

Yes. Form 26AS remains important for TDS/TCS and tax payment credit reconciliation.
