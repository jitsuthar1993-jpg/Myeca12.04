# PMJJBY Insurance Renewal and Nominee Checklist for 2026

Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) is one of India's most accessible life cover options — a ₹2 lakh term policy available at a very low annual premium through your savings bank account. But low cost does not mean zero maintenance. Every year, the auto-debit needs to go through on time, the nominee record needs to match the bank file, and the account must have sufficient balance. This guide is for people who are checking their renewal status or nominee details ahead of the May-June debit window in 2026. It does not guarantee eligibility or claim approval; that rests with your bank, the scheme rules, and the official portal.

## Who needs this checklist

PMJJBY covers individuals aged 18 to 50 years with a savings bank account linked to the scheme. The annual premium is auto-debited each year between 25 May and 31 May (the exact date may vary by bank). People who search for this checklist in 2026 are usually in one of three situations: they want to confirm the debit has gone through, they discovered their nominee name is wrong or outdated, or they are preparing a claim after a policyholder's death.

Whichever situation applies, the starting point is the same — go to the official source, pull the relevant documents, and fix any mismatch before relying on the record.

## Quick checklist

- Check the bank statement for the PMJJBY premium debit around 25–31 May 2026.
- Confirm the nominee name in the bank's records matches the nominee form submitted at the time of enrolment.
- Verify that the savings account linked to PMJJBY is active and not dormant.
- Cross-check identity documents — Aadhaar and PAN — for name and date-of-birth consistency.
- If a claim is being prepared, gather the official death certificate, nominee identity proof, and the bank account details of the claimant.
- Do not act on WhatsApp forwards or social media posts. Go to the official PMJDY portal or your bank's branch.

## Documents to keep ready

| Document | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Bank account passbook or statement | Confirms the annual premium debit has gone through and the account is active. |
| Nominee details (enrolment form copy) | The nominee name and relationship must match what the bank holds on record. |
| Premium debit proof | Bank SMS, passbook entry, or mini-statement confirming the debit for the current policy year. |
| Identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN) | Required for identity matching during claim settlement or record correction. |
| PAN and bank details | Needed for tax filing, income matching, and identity verification where applicable. |
| A short review note | Records what you checked, what is still pending, and which official source you used. |

## Practical example

Imagine a subscriber who notices a message from her bank in late May 2026 saying the PMJJBY debit failed due to insufficient balance. If she does nothing, the policy lapses for that year and no claim will be honoured during the lapse period. The right steps: top up the account before the cut-off date, ask the bank whether a late-debit window is available, and then get a written confirmation once the premium is deducted. She also checks whether the nominee — her husband — is still correctly recorded, because she had changed banks two years ago and was not sure the nominee detail carried over.

That kind of mismatch — nominee detail left behind when an account migrated — is exactly the type of problem that is easy to fix beforehand and very difficult to fix during a claim.

## Reviewer guidance for PMJJBY cases

When reviewing a PMJJBY file, confirm: the bank account linked to the scheme, the premium debit status for the current year, the nominee name and relationship as held by the bank, and any pending correction requests. If the case involves a claim, note the date of death, the claimant's relationship to the policyholder, and whether the death certificate is from a registered authority.

Where the review also touches AY 2026-27 ITR filing — for instance, if insurance proceeds or lapsed-premium treatment needs to be addressed — separately note the income head, ITR form applicable, tax regime chosen, and e-verification status.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is PMJJBY eligibility guaranteed by this guide?

No. Eligibility depends on the official portal, current scheme rules, state or ministry verification, and the applicant's documents.

### Should I use only social media information before applying?

No. Use social posts only to identify the issue, then verify the rule and application status on official government sources.

### Why keep tax records for a government scheme?

Many applications ask for income, bank, identity, or business records. A clean document trail reduces avoidable mismatch and follow-up questions.

## Final takeaway

PMJJBY's value — ₹2 lakh life cover at a very low annual premium — is only realised if the renewal goes through and the nominee record is correct. These are not complicated tasks, but they require checking the right sources at the right time. Use this checklist once a year, around May, to make sure both are in order.
