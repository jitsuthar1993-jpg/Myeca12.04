# PPF Account Tax Record Checklist for AY 2026-27

A PPF tax file should reconcile passbook entries, deposit receipts, interest information, withdrawals or loans, and the deduction amount used in the return. Keep account transactions by financial year so a tax claim is not based on the wrong period.



## Decide which PPF transactions belong to the tax working

A PPF tax file for AY 2026-27 should begin with the relevant financial-year transactions, not with the account's lifetime total. Identify the account holder, account number or office reference, deposits made during the applicable period, interest information, withdrawals or loans, and the deduction amount proposed for the return. Reconcile passbook entries, receipts, and bank transactions so the tax working uses the correct year and does not count a failed, reversed, or differently dated deposit.

Keep deposits separate from interest, withdrawals, loans, and transfers. Each has a different role in the account history and tax working. Do not infer a deduction from an intended payment or from a receipt belonging to another period. Review the current tax form and applicable instructions before using the reconciled amount, and avoid presenting the account balance as the deductible contribution.

## Keep account records and tax-return entries under their owners

The account holder owns the accuracy of the personal tax working. The bank or post office controls the PPF passbook, account transactions, and recognised corrections. The bank used to fund a deposit controls its debit record, while the tax portal controls the filed return and processing messages. A bank debit that never reached the PPF account should not be treated as a completed deposit, and a passbook correction does not automatically amend a filed return.

Preserve deposit receipts, passbook entries, funding-account evidence where needed, interest and withdrawal records, and the return working. If a transaction is missing or wrong, raise it with the account operator and retain the acknowledgement and corrected entry. If the tax return used an incorrect amount, address the return through the applicable tax route rather than asking the PPF operator to change an accurate account record.

## Reconcile the account and return in dated sequence

Create a chronology for the financial year showing each deposit request, funding debit, PPF posting, reversal or correction, and receipt. Then link the supported total to the deduction working and filed return. Keep interest, withdrawals, or loans in their own sequence so later account questions do not alter the contribution calculation without reason.

If a tax query or account discrepancy arises, identify the exact transaction and period. Preserve the response and any revised working without overwriting the original return file. At year-end, retain the account statement or passbook position and the calculation used. The final trail should let the taxpayer demonstrate which deposits were actually posted for the relevant year, how the return amount was derived, and how any correction progressed without using a lifetime balance or unrelated transaction as proof.

Before finalising the return, mark each claimed deposit as reconciled, pending, reversed, or excluded and record the reason. A pending account correction should remain visible rather than being counted optimistically. If a deposit posts after the date first expected, preserve both transaction dates and use the applicable tax instruction to determine the correct period. This disciplined close prevents the same receipt from being counted twice or a missing posting from being hidden inside the annual total.
