# PM Fasal Bima Yojana Claim Document Checklist for 2026

A crop-insurance claim should be tied to the insured season, crop, plot or activity, policy acknowledgement, loss intimation, and bank account. Keep proof of the intimation date and later survey or insurer communication; a general crop record cannot replace a claim chronology.


Build a crop-insurance claim around the insured event and its dates. Connect the relevant season, crop, plot or activity, policy or enrolment acknowledgement, premium or coverage record, loss intimation, survey or assessment communication, bank account, and claim response. Record when and how the loss was reported and preserve every later request. A land record or general crop detail can support identity or activity, but it does not establish the insured event, timely intimation, assessed loss, or claim outcome.

## Define the insured event before building the claim

A crop-insurance claim must be tied to the insured season, crop, plot or activity, policy or enrolment acknowledgement, and the event said to have caused loss. Begin by identifying those facts and the date on which the loss was noticed. Then check the applicable intimation route and the information it requests. A general land record, crop photograph, or bank entry may support part of the file, but none alone establishes coverage, timely intimation, assessed loss, or claim outcome.

Describe the event accurately and preserve evidence created at the time. Do not change dates, crop details, plot references, or damage descriptions to fit a later understanding. If policy or enrolment information itself is wrong, identify that separately from the loss event and use the appropriate correction or grievance route. The claim should remain readable as an event-specific record.

## Keep coverage, land, survey, and bank facts separate

The insurer and applicable programme channel own policy, intimation, survey, assessment, and claim-status records. Land or crop-record authorities own their source entries. The farmer owns the accuracy of information supplied and evidence preserved. The bank controls premium or claim transaction entries. A bank debit may support payment history, but it does not prove the insured event. A land entry may support the plot or activity, yet it does not prove the date or extent of loss.

Preserve the policy or enrolment acknowledgement, proof and time of intimation, event evidence, survey or assessment communication, bank details, and every claim response. If a source record or policy field is disputed, keep the original and correction request visible. Do not replace the initial loss description with a later survey result; both belong in the chronology and may answer different questions.

## Follow the claim from intimation to final response

Build the sequence around dates: coverage or enrolment, insured season, event, first notice, formal intimation, acknowledgement, survey or assessment, requests for information, response, decision, and payment or grievance. Record how each message was delivered and retain its reference. When status appears stalled, ask which claim stage is pending and who owns the next action.

For a payment query, connect the claim response and transaction reference to the receiving account without treating the bank statement as the decision record. For a dispute, identify the exact policy, event, field, or assessment being challenged and preserve the grievance response. The finished file should let another reader understand what was insured, what happened, when it was reported, and how the insurer responded without reconstructing the event from unrelated farm documents.

Before escalating a disputed assessment, compare the initial loss description, survey communication, claim response, and field or crop records used. State the exact disagreement and the evidence supporting it without rewriting the original intimation. Record any weather, authority, or local evidence only if it is genuine and relevant to the route. This keeps the grievance focused on the insured event and assessed claim rather than a broad account of farm hardship.

## PM Fasal Bima Yojana Claim: source pages and next actions

Read [Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana official portal](https://pmfby.gov.in/) for the current instruction affecting recordkeeping for crop, bank, insurance, and loss-intimation records. Keep that PM Fasal Bima Yojana Claim page and its check date with the application record, and route an error in the underlying source to the issuer or programme channel that owns the disputed fact.
