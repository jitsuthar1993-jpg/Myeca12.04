# PAN Card Application, Correction, and Business PAN Readiness

PAN is the tax identity behind many business workflows. It appears in bank onboarding, GST registration, TDS records, MCA filings, Udyam, invoices, investment records, and tax filings. A mismatch in PAN details can slow down multiple services at once.

Before applying or correcting PAN, treat the work as identity cleanup.

## Decide the PAN requirement

Confirm whether the case is new PAN, instant e-PAN, duplicate PAN, correction, entity PAN, or proprietor onboarding. A proprietor's PAN position is different from a company or LLP, so do not copy one checklist for every case.

## Match records before filing

Compare name, address, date of birth or incorporation, Aadhaar context, bank details, GST, MCA records, Udyam, invoices, and TDS records. If two records use different spelling or old addresses, resolve the source of truth before submitting a correction.

## Document file

Keep KYC proof, entity proof, address proof, old PAN copy if available, bank records, GST or MCA records, and acknowledgement documents together.
