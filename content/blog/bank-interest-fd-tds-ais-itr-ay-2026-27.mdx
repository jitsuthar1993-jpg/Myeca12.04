# Bank Interest, FD TDS, AIS, and ITR Filing for AY 2026-27

Bank interest and FD TDS should be matched with AIS, Form 16A, and Form 26AS before AY 2026-27 ITR filing or refund claim.

This guide is for taxpayers filing FY 2025-26 income in AY 2026-27. Most filing errors do not begin at the submit button. They begin earlier — when someone picks the wrong assessment year, trusts a prefill without checking it, treats AIS and Form 26AS as the same document, or reaches for ITR-1 when a different form is actually needed.

Read this as a practical review note before you file. It does not guarantee a refund, faster processing, or protection from a notice. What it does is help you organise the numbers, pick the right route, and recognise when a CA's input would be sensible.

## Bank interest ITR: points that change the filing

| Point | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Interest income must be reported even when TDS is deducted. |
| 2 | Form 16A and Form 26AS should match. |
| 3 | AIS may show interest not visible in salary records. |

## Current rule behind bank interest ITR

The Income Tax Department's guidance on tax credits is clear: Form 26AS includes TDS deducted and taxes paid, but the related income must also appear in the return. Showing the credit without the income is not an acceptable filing position.

Treat the portal, notified forms, and validation rules as the rule source. Blog articles — this one included — are a starting point for understanding, not a substitute for checking your own documents against the law.

## Bank interest ITR: worked example

Consider a retiree with FD interest of Rs 1,80,000 and TDS already deducted by the bank. The right approach is to report the full interest amount, claim the matching TDS credit, and verify that both entries appear correctly in AIS before expecting any refund.

Work through the situation in three stages. First, confirm the assessment year and identify the taxpayer's profile. Second, determine which income head applies, which ITR form is required, and which schedule carries the interest. Third, reconcile the tax credit against Form 16A, Form 26AS, and AIS. If any stage throws up a mismatch, stop and resolve it before submitting. That is where notices, refund delays, and defective-return problems typically take root.

## Bank interest TDS: Build the evidence file for bank interest TDS

- Bank interest certificate
- Form 16A
- AIS and TIS
- Form 26AS
- Bank statement

Keep these in a single folder with a short computation note. Even a plain-language note — "this number appears in the return because..." — is worth writing. It becomes invaluable months later when a Form 16 amount, an AIS entry, or a bank interest figure needs to be explained.

## Bank interest ITR filing checks

- For bank interest TDS, confirm AY 2026-27 is the correct assessment year for FY 2025-26 income.
- Match the income to the correct head and the correct return form.
- In the bank interest TDS working, compare Form 16 or Form 16A with AIS, TIS, Form 26AS, and bank records.
- Make a note for every mismatch, correction, deduction, refund position, or notice-sensitive entry.
- File only when the figures are supportable, then e-verify within the allowed window.

## Risks to resolve before bank interest ITR

- Claiming TDS credit without reporting the interest income.
- Overlooking savings-account interest entirely.
- Filing before the bank's TDS deduction has appeared on Form 26AS or AIS.
- Using a closed or inactive bank account as the refund account.

The costliest mistake is often choosing the wrong correction route. For bank interest TDS, a revised return, a rectification request, AIS feedback, ITR-U, a demand payment, a grievance, or a notice reply each solve distinct problems. Do not reach for whichever option is most visible on the portal; pick the one that fits the situation and the statutory window.
