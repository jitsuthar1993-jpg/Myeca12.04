# AY 2026-27 ESOP Sale and Perquisite ITR Checklist

If you sold ESOP shares during the year, your return has to tell two related stories at once: the perquisite that was taxed when you exercised the options, and the capital gain or loss when you eventually sold the shares. Joining those two correctly is where most employees stumble. This guide walks through that for FY 2025-26 income being filed for AY 2026-27. Treat it as a careful filing checklist, not a refund promise, a processing-time guarantee, or a reason to skip the official utility.

## Who this is for

People landing on this topic are usually employees selling ESOP shares who recognise the broad situation but are not sure which documents to gather, which portal to trust, or what to do first. The one habit worth keeping is to never paste a stranger's answer straight into your return. Begin with official sources, then assemble a document file you can actually defend later.

For MyeCA readers that boils down to three things. Keep the assessment-year or scheme-year context straight. Make sure names, PAN, Aadhaar, bank account, and income figures line up before you submit anything. And hold on to the acknowledgement or application reference, because chasing a later refund, subsidy, loan, or notice without it is a real headache.

## Quick checklist

- Confirm the official portal or Income Tax Department source before acting.
- Keep identity, bank, income, and scheme-specific documents in one folder.
- Match AIS, Form 26AS, Form 16, Form 16A, GST, or bank records where they apply.
- Do not assume eligibility, refund, subsidy, or loan approval without official verification.
- Use a CA or expert review where the record affects tax, business compliance, or high-value benefits.

## A worked example

Picture someone who searches for "ESOP sale ITR AY 2026-27" after a portal message, an employer record, a bank credit, or an application asking for proof. Rather than rushing to file or apply, this person first pulls together the documents, opens the official source links below, and jots down a short note of the facts as they stand. The moment a name, bank, Aadhaar, PAN, income, or TDS figure does not agree across documents, they stop and resolve that gap before trusting the record.

Yes, this takes longer than copying a quick generic answer. It is also far safer. The trouble in most tax and scheme cases rarely comes from the headline rule; it comes from small details that disagree with each other across documents.

## Review notes for employees selling ESOP shares

Whoever reviews the file should record the user profile, the official source that was checked, the documents seen, any mismatch still open, and the next action. When the file feeds into AY 2026-27 ITR filing, the note should also capture the income head, the ITR form, the tax regime, the TDS or TCS credit, and the e-verification status. When it relates to a government scheme instead, the note should call out the scheme portal, the application reference, the eligibility documents, and whether the bank account is ready to receive credit.
