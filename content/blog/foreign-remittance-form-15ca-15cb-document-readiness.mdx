# Foreign Remittance Form 15CA and 15CB Document Readiness

Foreign remittance compliance is a document and taxability exercise. Before a bank processes a remittance, the payer may need to evaluate Form 15CA, Form 15CB, TDS, DTAA, purpose code, and supporting evidence.

This work should be handled before the payment date, not after the bank asks for documents.

## Start with remittance facts

Record the remitter, remittee, country, amount, currency, purpose, invoice, agreement, proposed date, and whether the payment is chargeable to tax in India. This determines the Form 15CA part and whether Form 15CB coordination is needed.

## Prepare documents

Keep invoice, agreement, bank details, PAN, tax residency certificate where relevant, Form 10F context where relevant, lower-deduction certificate if any, CA assignment details, and remittance purpose note.

## Form 15CB coordination

Form 15CB is a CA certificate route for specified cases. It should not be assumed for every remittance, and it should not be skipped where facts require it. Preserve the certificate and acknowledgement trail.
