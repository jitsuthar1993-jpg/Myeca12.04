# PM Vishwakarma Registration and Document Checklist

A PM Vishwakarma registration should accurately describe the artisan or craft activity and use the applicant's own Aadhaar, mobile, bank, and local-verification records. Keep training, toolkit, credit, or later programme records separate from the initial registration.

The central factual question is whether the applicant actually practises the trade entered in the registration. Keep local-verification or trade evidence that explains that activity, but do not invent business history merely to fit a category. Training, toolkit support, credit, and marketing assistance are later programme stages; record them separately from the initial artisan-registration evidence.

Keep artisan registration, local verification, training, toolkit support, credit, and marketing assistance as distinct stages. The initial file should explain the trade actually practised and the evidence used for that classification. Later programme records should identify the stage, authority or provider, date, and outcome. Do not create business history or invoices merely to fit a trade label, and do not treat completion of one stage as proof that another benefit has been approved.

## Describe the artisan's actual trade before registration

The central PM Vishwakarma registration decision is whether the applicant genuinely practises the trade entered in the form. Describe the work performed, tools used, customer or work context, location, and whether the activity is current. Use the applicant's own Aadhaar, mobile, bank, and local-verification information as requested through the official route. Do not invent business history, invoices, customers, or a different craft merely to fit a listed trade.

Review the identity and contact details with the artisan, especially when another person assists with registration. The initial file should explain why the selected trade accurately describes the work. It should not assume that registration automatically establishes training completion, toolkit support, credit, marketing assistance, or any later programme stage.

## Separate artisan registration from later support stages

The artisan owns the factual trade description and personal details supplied. Aadhaar and banking providers remain responsible for their own source records. The local-verification and programme routes control registration and recognised status. Training providers, toolkit channels, lenders, and marketing-support routes each control their later-stage records. Completion or communication at one stage does not prove that another stage has been approved or delivered.

Preserve the original registration, trade description, local-verification response, and every update acknowledgement. If the trade or personal information is wrong, identify whether the source record or programme profile needs correction and keep both versions. For later training, toolkit, or credit activity, open a separate record showing the provider or authority, date, reference, and outcome. Do not fold a loan appraisal or training attendance issue into a general registration grievance.

## Follow the artisan through each recognised programme stage

Build a chronology beginning with trade description and registration. Add local verification, displayed status, training invitations and attendance, assessment or completion records, toolkit communication, credit application and lender response, and any later support. Label each stage by its owner and actual outcome. This makes it possible to identify a missing action without claiming that registration promised every later benefit.

When information changes, record the effective date and acknowledgement from the relevant owner. Preserve unsuccessful, declined, or pending stages as part of the history. If a dispute arises, cite the stage-specific reference and evidence rather than resubmitting an undifferentiated artisan file. The completed trail should show what trade the applicant practised, how registration was verified, and what happened at each later stage without fabricating credentials or treating one completed action as approval of the next.

Before moving from registration into training, toolkit, credit, or marketing support, compare the artisan's current trade and contact details with the recognised profile. Record the invitation or instruction for that stage and what action the artisan chose. If the trade has genuinely changed, seek a profile correction rather than presenting a new activity as old experience. This stage-entry note makes later attendance, delivery, lender, or support questions easier to direct and keeps the artisan's history truthful.

## PM Vishwakarma: source pages and next actions

Read [PM Vishwakarma official portal](https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/) for the current instruction affecting trade category, identity, bank account, and local verification readiness. Keep that PM Vishwakarma page and its check date with the application record, and route an error in the underlying source to the issuer or programme channel that owns the disputed fact.
