# ESI and EPFO Registration: Employer Payroll Readiness Guide

ESI and EPFO compliance depends on accurate payroll records. A growing employer should review applicability when hiring begins, when employee count changes, when wage levels change, or when operations expand to a new workplace.

Registration is useful only when the employer can maintain monthly records afterwards.

## Build the employee base

Prepare employee master data with name, PAN, Aadhaar context where applicable, date of joining, wage details, bank account, work location, designation, and contact details. Keep appointment letters, salary structure, attendance, and leave records aligned.

## Employer registration file

Keep entity proof, PAN, bank details, workplace proof, authorized signatory details, DSC or login custody notes, employee list, and wage records. If there are multiple branches or units, document the structure clearly.

## Monthly workflow

After registration, track contributions, challans, returns, employee additions, exits, wage changes, and inspection communication. Preserve every acknowledgement.
