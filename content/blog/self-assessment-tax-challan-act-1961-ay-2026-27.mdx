# Self-Assessment Tax Challan for AY 2026-27: Select Act 1961

For AY 2026-27 self-assessment tax, official portal guidance says Income Tax Act, 1961 applies. Check challan, PAN, amount, and return credit.

One small but consequential detail trips up filers every year: selecting the wrong Act when generating a self-assessment tax challan. With the Income-tax Act, 2025 now in the picture, the question of which statute governs AY 2026-27 self-assessment has become more common. The official portal answers it clearly — for Self Assessment Tax for AY 2026-27, the Income Tax Act, 1961 applies. This guide helps you act on that correctly.

| --- | --- |

## Official position for self assessment 2026-27

The Income Tax Department's general questions section confirms that Self Assessment Tax for AY 2026-27 falls under the Income Tax Act, 1961. If you see the 2025 Act listed as an option on a challan page, do not pick it for AY 2026-27 self-assessment tax. Treat the official portal as the rule source, not third-party blogs or tax calculators.

## Example using self assessment 2026-27 records

A taxpayer computes additional tax liability before filing their AY 2026-27 return. They generate a challan under the Income Tax Act, 1961, save the receipt, and then verify that the CIN, date, and amount match the tax-paid schedule in the return preview before submitting. That three-step sequence — pay, save, reconcile — prevents most demand notices on this point.

Go through the exercise in three passes. First, confirm the assessment year and taxpayer profile. Second, identify the income head, the right ITR form, and the applicable schedule. Third, match tax credits and supporting documents. If any pass throws up a discrepancy, pause. Notices, refund delays, and defective-return flags almost always trace back to a gap that existed at this stage.

## Self assessment challan: Build the evidence file for self assessment 2026-27

- Challan receipt
- PAN and assessment year details
- Computation sheet
- Form 26AS or tax payment record
- Return preview

Keep all of these in one folder, alongside a brief computation note explaining how the tax payable figure was arrived at. When a Form 16 amount, AIS entry, or bank interest figure needs to be justified later, that note saves significant time.

## Before filing self assessment 2026-27

- For self assessment challan, confirm AY 2026-27 is the correct assessment year for FY 2025-26 income.
- Match the challan to the correct income head and return form.
- In the self assessment challan working, compare Form 16 or Form 16A with AIS, TIS, Form 26AS, bank records, and any broker or business records.
- Note every mismatch, correction, deduction, refund position, or notice-sensitive entry.
- Complete self assessment challan filing only after the figures are supportable, then e-verify within the allowed timeline.

## Self assessment challan: Errors that can change self assessment 2026-27

- Selecting the Income-tax Act, 2025 instead of the 1961 Act when generating a challan for AY 2026-27.
- Entering an incorrect PAN, which prevents credit from flowing into the return.
- Quoting a wrong challan number or date in the tax-paid schedule.
- Submitting the return before checking that the payment credit is actually reflected.

The costliest error is picking the wrong resolution route after the fact. For self assessment challan, a revised return, a rectification request, an AIS feedback, an ITR-U, a demand payment, a grievance, and a notice reply each address a different problem. Choosing the right route depends on knowing exactly what went wrong and when.
