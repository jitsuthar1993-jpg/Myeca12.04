# Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY Card Eligibility Checklist for Families

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY provides health cover of up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation at empanelled hospitals across India. For families who qualify, the Ayushman card is effectively free health insurance — but eligibility is determined by the beneficiary database built from the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC), not by self-declaration. This means the first step is always checking whether your family appears in that database, not filling an application form.

This guide walks through that verification process and the documents you should keep ready before approaching a hospital or Common Service Centre for card generation.

## How eligibility actually works

PM-JAY eligibility is pre-determined. The government identified beneficiary families based on SECC data and linked some additional categories. You cannot apply and get added if your family was not already in the database. What you can do is verify whether your family is listed, and if so, generate the Ayushman card using your Aadhaar and ration card details.

Check your eligibility at pmjay.gov.in by entering your mobile number or ration card number. If your family appears, proceed to the nearest empanelled hospital or CSC for card generation. If your family does not appear and you believe you should be included, the process for grievance or correction is different from the main card application — consult the official portal or state health agency.

## What to carry for card generation

The card generation process requires identity verification. Carry your Aadhaar, the ration card under which your family is listed, and a mobile number that is active and in your name or the primary applicant's name. If any family member's name on the ration card does not match their Aadhaar, that specific member may face verification delays — address the mismatch before visiting.

## Hospital network: check before admission

Not every hospital accepts PM-JAY. The scheme covers treatment at empanelled hospitals listed on the PMJAY portal. Before planning a hospitalisation, confirm the hospital is on the empanelled list for the relevant procedure. Cashless treatment is available only at these hospitals — if you go to a non-empanelled hospital, you will not get the benefit, even if you have an Ayushman card.

In an emergency, show the Ayushman card at any empanelled hospital you can reach. Empanelled hospitals are required to provide pre-authorised cashless treatment to eligible beneficiaries.

## For the tax advisor or reviewer

PM-JAY benefits are not taxable income. However, if the family has also purchased private health insurance, ensure the Section 80D deduction claim in the AY 2026-27 ITR reflects only premiums actually paid out of pocket — not notional or anticipated amounts. If medical bills were partly reimbursed through PM-JAY, only the portion paid from personal funds is eligible for 80D. Keep the hospitalisation documents and the PM-JAY authorisation letter together so the out-of-pocket component is clear if the return is scrutinised.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY eligibility guaranteed by this guide?

No. Eligibility depends on the official portal, current scheme rules, state or ministry verification, and the applicant's documents.

### Should I use only social media information before applying?

No. Use social posts only to identify the issue, then verify the rule and application status on official government sources.

### Why keep tax records for a government scheme?

Many applications ask for income, bank, identity, or business records. A clean document trail reduces avoidable mismatch and follow-up questions.

## Key takeaway

PM-JAY eligibility is not applied for — it is verified. Check the database first. If your family is listed, the card generation is a single visit to an empanelled hospital or CSC. If there is a name mismatch or a database discrepancy, resolve that separately through the official grievance process before expecting cashless treatment to work smoothly.
