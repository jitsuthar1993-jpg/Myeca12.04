# PMKVY Skill Training Registration Checklist for Job Seekers

Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) provides free short-term skill training to youth across India through certified training partners. The scheme covers hundreds of job roles across sectors such as construction, electronics, healthcare, retail, and IT. For a job seeker in 2026, the key questions are practical: which training centre is empanelled near me, which course matches my profile, what documents do I bring, and how do I ensure the certificate I earn is recognised? This guide walks through exactly that — the documents, the verification steps, and where to confirm details officially. It does not promise placement, stipend, or loan approval.

## Who is looking for this information

Most people searching for PMKVY registration documents in 2026 fall into three groups: young people looking to enrol in a free course for the first time, workers who want to get their existing skills formally certified under Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL), and those who completed a PMKVY course earlier and need their certificate details for a job application or further training.

Each group needs slightly different documents. A fresh enrolment requires basic identity and educational records. RPL needs proof of work experience. A certificate lookup needs the original enrolment reference or candidate ID. The starting point for all three is the official Skill India Digital portal, not a local agent's advice or a social media post.

## Quick checklist

- Locate an empanelled Training Centre through the Skill India Digital portal — do not register through unverified agents.
- Check that the course you want is currently active under PMKVY 4.0 and that the training centre has a valid accreditation.
- Confirm your Aadhaar is active and the mobile number linked to it is working — the portal uses Aadhaar-based registration.
- Carry your original education certificates (even if the course has no minimum qualification requirement, the centre may record them).
- If you need a bank account for stipend credit, make sure the account is in your own name and Aadhaar-linked.
- After training, collect the PMKVY certificate and keep a copy of the assessment result slip separately.

## Documents to keep ready

| Document | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Aadhaar | Mandatory for candidate registration on the Skill India Digital portal; name and mobile number must be consistent. |
| Mobile number (Aadhaar-linked) | OTP-based verification during enrolment requires the registered mobile to be active. |
| Education proof | Mark sheets or school-leaving certificate may be required by the training centre for record purposes. |
| Bank details (if applicable) | Required for stipend credit or post-placement support where the course qualifies. |
| PAN and bank details | Useful for tax filing, income matching, and identity verification where applicable. |
| A short review note | Records what you checked, what is still pending, and which official source you used. |

## Practical example

Consider a 22-year-old from a semi-urban area who sees a PMKVY advertisement and wants to enrol in a solar technician course. A common mistake is approaching a local operator who charges a "registration fee" — PMKVY training is free, and any centre asking for money upfront is either unregistered or operating improperly. The correct step is to visit the Skill India Digital portal, search for accredited training centres in the district, and verify that the solar sector course is listed as active. Once the centre is confirmed, the candidate carries Aadhaar and education documents, registers biometrically, and receives an enrolment acknowledgement that should be preserved throughout the course.

At the end of training, the candidate completes an assessment conducted by a Sector Skill Council. The certificate issued after passing the assessment is the document that matters for employment. Keeping both the enrolment slip and the certificate together prevents problems later.

## Reviewer guidance for PMKVY cases

When reviewing a PMKVY file, confirm: the training centre's empanelment status, the course and sector, the candidate's Aadhaar-linked registration status, and whether any stipend payment was made and recorded. For candidates using PMKVY certification to support self-employment or a GST registration, cross-check the qualification against the business activity.

Where the review also touches AY 2026-27 ITR filing — for example, if the candidate is now earning from a newly started business linked to the skill — note the income head, ITR form applicable (likely ITR-4 for presumptive income), tax regime chosen, and e-verification status.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is PMKVY eligibility guaranteed by this guide?

No. Eligibility depends on the official portal, current scheme rules, state or ministry verification, and the applicant's documents.

### Should I use only social media information before applying?

No. Use social posts only to identify the issue, then verify the rule and application status on official government sources.

### Why keep tax records for a government scheme?

Many applications ask for income, bank, identity, or business records. A clean document trail reduces avoidable mismatch and follow-up questions.

## Final takeaway

PMKVY's benefit is a certified, assessor-verified skill credential — and it is entirely free. The main risk is not the scheme itself but acting on unverified information or approaching unregistered training centres. Check the official portal, collect the right documents, and keep the enrolment and assessment records safely after you complete the course.
