# PMKVY Skill Training Registration Checklist for Job Seekers

A PMKVY learner should check the course, training centre, identity route, mobile access, and education proof before registration. Preserve attendance, assessment, and certificate records later; a registration acknowledgement alone does not establish completion or placement.

Verify the course and training centre before treating registration as an employment outcome. Keep attendance, assessment, certification, and placement communication by stage so the learner can show what was completed and can challenge a missing or incorrect result.

## Choose the course and centre before treating registration as progress

A PMKVY learner should first decide whether the course and training centre fit the intended skill path. Identify the course title, centre, location, schedule, identity route, required education evidence, and contact details used for registration. Verify the centre and course through the current recognised channel before supplying sensitive records or making commitments. Registration only establishes an entry into the process; it does not prove attendance, assessment, certification, or placement.

Review the learner's education and identity records for accurate names and dates. Use a mobile number the learner can access throughout training. Do not invent education, experience, attendance, or skill level to fit a course. If the course description, batch timing, or centre information differs from what was presented, seek clarification before assuming the registration will lead to the expected training.

## Assign training, assessment, and placement records correctly

The learner owns the accuracy of personal information and participation. Education issuers own their certificates. The training centre controls its batch, schedule, attendance, and training communication. The assessment or certification route controls assessment and certificate records, while an employer controls any placement or job offer. A registration correction cannot repair missing attendance, and a certificate does not prove employment.

Preserve the registration acknowledgement, course and centre details, batch communication, attendance records available to the learner, assessment messages, certificate, and any placement communication. When a field or result is disputed, identify the stage and contact its owner. Keep the earlier record and the correction response together. Do not allow a centre or intermediary to retain original credentials unnecessarily or represent a placement discussion as a confirmed job.

## Trace the learner through each training milestone

Create a dated chronology beginning with course verification and registration. Add batch confirmation, training start, attendance concerns, completion, assessment scheduling, assessment result, certificate availability, and any placement interaction. For a missing or incorrect result, state the learner, course, centre, batch, date, and disputed event. This gives the responsible owner enough context to answer without resubmitting unrelated records.

If the learner changes contact details or leaves the course, preserve the update or exit record and its effective date. At the end, confirm training completion, assessment, certification, and placement as separate outcomes. A complete file should show what the learner registered for, what training occurred, who assessed it, and what followed, without turning the first acknowledgement into a claim of completion or employment.

Before raising a complaint, the learner should prepare a batch-specific summary showing scheduled sessions, attendance actually recorded, assessment date or missing assessment, and the centre's latest response. If the dispute concerns a certificate, compare the displayed name, course, result, and issue details with the training and assessment records. If it concerns placement, identify the employer interaction and outcome separately. This summary directs the matter to the right stage and avoids making a broad employment claim from a training registration.

## PMKVY Skill Training: source pages and next actions

Read [Skill India Digital official portal](https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in/) for the current instruction affecting identity, training centre, course fit, and certificate records. Keep that PMKVY Skill Training page and its check date with the application record, and route an error in the underlying source to the issuer or programme channel that owns the disputed fact.
