# PMFME Food Processing Scheme Checklist for Micro Units

PMFME Food Processing Scheme Checklist for Micro Units starts with three concrete checks: whether business KYC identifies the right applicant, project estimate supports the claimed condition, and bank statement fits the current instructions on PMFME official portal.

## food business records case check: reconstruct the answer

Test the PMFME application before portal entry: ask which live instruction is answered by business KYC, which fact is confirmed by project estimate, and why food business records belongs with the result.

Test the application answer against business KYC, project estimate, and bank statement. If bank statement supports a different applicant fact, preserve the issue and request clarification before submission.

## PMFME: confirm the live route and applicant fit

A useful PMFME application note for food processing micro units names the intended outcome, the live route, the fact supported by business KYC, and the issue that project estimate or bank statement must settle.

## Food processing micro units: map business KYC to food business records

The PMFME application record map begins with business KYC and ends with food business records; project estimate and bank statement should resolve separate questions rather than repeat the same unsupported answer.

| Record | What it must support |
| --- | --- |
| business KYC | Business KYC should support the live application answer about applicant identity. For PMFME scheme documents 2026, use the format and issuing record named in the current authority instruction. |
| project estimate | Project estimate should support activity, cost, or funding details exactly as entered. Compare any different name, date, identifier, or status with bank statement before submission. |
| bank statement | Use bank statement for payment-account details; use food business records separately for trade, occupation, or business-activity details. Resolve any PMFME scheme documents 2026 conflict through source-record correction or written clarification. |
| food business records | Food business records belong in the file because they support trade, occupation, or business-activity details. Confirm the issuer, validity, applicant details, and relevant period before relying on food business records. |

## bank statement conflict: decide before upload

A material difference between business KYC and project estimate can change the PMFME application; record the affected answer, the owner of the correction, and the limited role of bank statement.

Check whether business KYC and project estimate describe the same applicant, period, and activity. If not, pause the application; use bank statement only for payment-account details.

## Food processing micro units: verify the authority route

Use PMFME official portal to confirm the current route for food processing micro units, then record which official page answers the remaining bank statement and food business records questions.

| Official reference | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| [myScheme - official government scheme discovery portal](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/) | Record the department link used by food processing micro units; reopen it before preparing the final business KYC upload. |
| [PMFME official portal](https://pmfme.mofpi.gov.in/) | Verify the conditions, document format, submission route, and follow-up channel that apply to project estimate for food processing micro units. |

## PMFME application: evidence limits and retention

PMFME remains subject to current authority checks even after the application; preserve business KYC, food business records, and later clarification requests until the matter is closed.

Use the checked PMFME official portal route for the application, then keep business KYC, food business records, and the application acknowledgement in the same follow-up file.
