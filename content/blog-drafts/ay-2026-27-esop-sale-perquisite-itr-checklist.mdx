# AY 2026-27 ESOP Sale and Perquisite ITR Checklist

A reliable return for employees selling ESOP shares should be rebuildable from Form 16, ESOP exercise statement, and broker sale report; if those records support different answers, pause before selecting the form or schedule.

## Form 16 and ESOP exercise statement: decide the reporting treatment

The return should follow transactions supported by Form 16 and ESOP exercise statement. Check broker sale report for employees selling ESOP shares before deciding the amount and income classification.

1. Use Form 16 and ESOP exercise statement to settle the reporting treatment.
2. For employees selling ESOP shares, record that comparison in the computation before selecting a form or schedule.
3. Let broker sale report settle the open issue in the working before form selection.
4. After reconciling Form 16, place tax payment proof with the computation and acknowledgement.

## Form 16 and ESOP exercise statement: what each proves

Map amounts and dates to Form 16, then test credits and classifications against ESOP exercise statement. Preserve the named-record trail through filing.

| Record | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Form 16 | Form 16 should support salary, perquisites, deductions, and employer TDS; compare it with broker sale report. |
| ESOP exercise statement | ESOP exercise statement should show the amount, date, counterparty, and activity used in the filing working; compare it with tax payment proof. |
| broker sale report | Broker sale report should establish transaction dates, values, charges, and the resulting gain, loss, or holding-period treatment; compare it with Form 16. |
| tax payment proof | Tax payment proof should show the amount, date, counterparty, and activity used in the filing working; compare it with ESOP exercise statement. |

## Employees selling ESOP shares: reconcile broker sale report

Do not choose a transaction treatment from Form 16 alone. Compare it with ESOP exercise statement, resolve the difference through broker sale report, and retain tax payment proof with the recorded conclusion.

## Employees selling ESOP shares: worked check using tax payment proof

Compare a disputed Form 16 item with ESOP exercise statement, then use broker sale report to settle its amount and classification. Keep tax payment proof with the recorded conclusion.
