# AY 2026-27 Representative Filing Checklist for Deceased Taxpayers

Legal heirs and representatives need a return that can be traced back to the records, not merely a plausible prefilled figure. Start with death certificate and legal heir proof, then use income records to settle the classification, credit, deduction, or disclosure at issue.

## Death certificate and legal heir proof: decide the reporting treatment

Death certificate should establish the claimed amount or status, while legal heir proof tests a separate condition. Before deciding the return treatment, check income records for a conflicting taxpayer fact.

1. Use death certificate and legal heir proof to settle the reporting treatment.
2. For legal heirs and representatives, record that comparison in the computation before selecting a form or schedule.
3. Document how income records resolves the issue before choosing a form.
4. After reconciling death certificate, place bank details with the computation and acknowledgement.

## Death certificate and legal heir proof: what each proves

Assign a specific purpose to death certificate, legal heir proof, and every other return record. Do not let legal heir proof replace the fact established by death certificate.

## Legal heirs and representatives: reconcile income records

For legal heirs and representatives, resolve any conflict between the claim supported by death certificate and the condition shown by legal heir proof. Use income records to test the disputed condition, then retain bank details with the chosen treatment.

## Legal heirs and representatives: worked check using bank details

If death certificate supports a claim that legal heir proof does not, use income records to identify the unsupported condition. Retain bank details with the reason for the chosen treatment.
