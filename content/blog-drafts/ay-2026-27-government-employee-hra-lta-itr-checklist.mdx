# AY 2026-27 HRA and LTA Checklist for Government Employees

A reliable return for government employees should be rebuildable from Form 16, rent receipts, and travel bills; if those records support different answers, pause before selecting the form or schedule.

## Form 16 and rent receipts: decide the reporting treatment

Form 16 should establish the claimed amount or status, while rent receipts test a separate condition. Before deciding the return treatment, check travel bills for a conflicting taxpayer fact.

1. Use Form 16 and rent receipts to settle the reporting treatment.
2. For government employees, record that comparison in the computation before selecting a form or schedule.
3. Let travel bills settle the open issue in the working before form selection.
4. After reconciling Form 16, place salary breakup with the computation and acknowledgement.

Map amounts and dates to Form 16, then test credits and classifications against rent receipts. Preserve the named-record trail through filing.
