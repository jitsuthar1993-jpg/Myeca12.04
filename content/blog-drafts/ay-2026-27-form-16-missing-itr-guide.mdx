# AY 2026-27 ITR Guide When Form 16 Is Missing

Salary slips and bank salary credits describe different parts of file ITR without Form 16 AY 2026-27; connect them before deciding the reporting treatment, and retain Form 26AS beside the computation.

## Employees without Form 16: worked check using Form 26AS

If salary slips and bank salary credits describe one item differently, use AIS to trace its date, amount, and classification. Retain Form 26AS beside the reconciliation.

## Salary slips and bank salary credits: decide the reporting treatment

Start the transaction working with salary slips and bank salary credits. A difference shown by AIS must be resolved before selecting a form or schedule.

1. Use salary slips and bank salary credits to settle the reporting treatment.
2. For employees without Form 16, record that comparison in the computation before selecting a form or schedule.
3. AIS must resolve the open difference before form selection.
4. After reconciling salary slips, place Form 26AS with the computation and acknowledgement.

Salary slips and bank salary credits are not interchangeable evidence. Use the map below to identify what salary slips and bank salary credits each answer.

## Employees without Form 16: reconcile AIS

When salary slips and bank salary credits conflict, rebuild the disputed item from AIS. AIS establishes the amount and classification; keep Form 26AS with the return working.
