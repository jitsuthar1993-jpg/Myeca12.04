# PM SVANidhi Loan Checklist for Street Vendors

PM SVANidhi Loan Checklist for Street Vendors starts with three concrete checks: whether vendor certificate or reference identify the right applicant, bank account supports the claimed condition, and mobile number fits the current instructions on PM SVANidhi official portal.

## PM SVANidhi: confirm the live route and applicant fit

Before a PM SVANidhi finance application, street vendors should write down the intended outcome, open [PM SVANidhi official portal](https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in/), and decide whether the applicant, activity, funding need, and supporting records satisfy the live route from vendor certificate or reference, bank account, and mobile number.

## KYC case check: reconstruct the answer

PM SVANidhi is ready for a case check when street vendors can explain the applicant fact, the accepted source record, the open issue, and the purpose of KYC.

Suppose vendor certificate or reference supports the applicant detail but bank account shows a different period or status. Pause, verify the current authority instruction, and keep mobile number out of the upload until the conflict is resolved.

## Street vendors: map vendor certificate or reference to KYC

vendor certificate or reference, bank account, and mobile number each support a different part of PM SVANidhi loan documents 2026; do not use one record as a substitute for another.

| Record | What it must support |
| --- | --- |
| vendor certificate or reference | Vendor certificate or reference should answer the application question about consumer, premises, vendor, or cost details. Where it disagrees with bank account, identify the authoritative record before uploading. |
| bank account | Keep bank account with its issuer and date visible. It should support payment-account details without contradicting the verification and alert details shown by mobile number. |
| mobile number | Mobile number is useful only when the application can trace verification and alert details back to it. Resolve any incomplete or inconsistent PM SVANidhi loan documents 2026 detail before submission. |
| KYC | Verify applicant identity from KYC, then test the same applicant file against vendor certificate or reference. Save the checked PM SVANidhi loan documents 2026 evidence with the final acknowledgement. |

## mobile number conflict: decide before upload

A material difference between vendor certificate or reference and bank account can change the PM SVANidhi finance application; record the affected answer, the owner of the correction, and the limited role of mobile number.

Before the PM SVANidhi finance application, confirm vendor certificate or reference supports consumer, premises, vendor, or cost details. Bank account must separately support payment-account details; keep KYC with the application acknowledgement.

## Street vendors: verify the authority route

The reference table gives street vendors a source for each decision: discovery, current route, accepted vendor certificate or reference, and follow-up after the finance application.

| Official reference | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| [myScheme - official government scheme discovery portal](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/) | Confirm the administering authority for PM SVANidhi and its live department link before relying on the eligibility or document summary. |
| [PM SVANidhi official portal](https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in/) | Verify the conditions, document format, submission route, and follow-up channel that apply to bank account for street vendors. |

## PM SVANidhi finance application: evidence limits and retention

Location, applicant facts, and submission date may change the PM SVANidhi route for street vendors; unresolved ownership, legal, tax, or eligibility questions need authority guidance or suitable professional advice.

The PM SVANidhi file should end with a clear choice for street vendors: submit through the confirmed route, correct a source record, obtain accepted evidence, or ask the authority for clarification.
