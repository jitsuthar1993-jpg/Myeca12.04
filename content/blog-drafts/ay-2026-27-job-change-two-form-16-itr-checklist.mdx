# AY 2026-27 ITR Checklist for Employees With a Job Change

Employees who changed jobs need a return that can be traced back to the records, not merely a plausible prefilled figure. Start with both Form 16s and salary slips, then use AIS and Form 26AS to settle the classification, credit, deduction, or disclosure at issue.

## Employees who changed jobs: worked check using investment proofs

Where both Form 16s and salary slips do not reconcile, identify the matching entry in AIS and Form 26AS. Record the reason for its treatment and retain investment proofs.

## Both Form 16s and salary slips: decide the reporting treatment

Read both Form 16s and salary slips against the decision about which return treatment both Form 16s and salary slips support. Record how AIS and Form 26AS changes the treatment, and keep investment proofs with the final computation.

1. Use both Form 16s and salary slips to settle the reporting treatment.
2. For employees who changed jobs, record that comparison in the computation before selecting a form or schedule.
3. Document how AIS and Form 26AS resolves the issue before choosing a form.
4. After reconciling both Form 16s, place investment proofs with the computation and acknowledgement.

## Both Form 16s and salary slips: what each proves

Assign a specific purpose to both Form 16s, salary slips, and every other return record. Do not let salary slips replace the fact established by both Form 16s.

| Record | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| both Form 16s | Both Form 16s should support salary, perquisites, deductions, and employer TDS; compare it with AIS and Form 26AS. |
| salary slips | Salary slips should support the decision to combine salary from two employers and checking whether deductions or TDS are duplicated; reconcile it with investment proofs before filing. |
| AIS and Form 26AS | Use AIS and Form 26AS to identify third-party-reported entries, then investigate unsupported items against both Form 16s. |
| investment proofs | Investment proofs should establish transaction dates, values, charges, and the resulting gain, loss, or holding-period treatment; compare it with salary slips. |

## Employees who changed jobs: reconcile AIS and Form 26AS

Resolve a material difference between both Form 16s and salary slips before filing. Record the effect of AIS and Form 26AS on which return treatment both Form 16s and salary slips support, then retain investment proofs with that working.

AIS and Form 26AS determine which form and schedule need checking; record the relevant instruction from Income Tax Department - AY 2026-27 ITR utilities beside both Form 16s.

## Combining salary from two employers and checking whether deductions or TDS are duplicated: limits and pause points

The records available for employees who changed jobs cannot settle the return without complete facts; stop when they support different answers or the proposed form lacks a required schedule.

## Both Form 16s: final filing check

1. Employees who changed jobs should have one reporting treatment supported by the source records.
2. For employees who changed jobs, choose the form and schedules only after the material mismatch in AIS and Form 26AS is resolved in the computation.
3. For employees who changed jobs, retain investment proofs with the computation, submitted return, and acknowledgement.
