## Medical certificate and prescription records: what each proves

Medical certificate and prescription records are not interchangeable evidence. Use the map below to identify what medical certificate and prescription records each answer.

| Record | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| medical certificate | Medical certificate should establish the amount, period, taxpayer fact, and issuer used in the working; compare it with dependent details. |
| prescription records | Prescription records should show the amount, date, counterparty, and activity used in the filing working; compare it with medical certificate. |
| payment proof | Payment proof should show the amount, date, counterparty, and activity used in the filing working; compare it with prescription records. |
| dependent details | Dependent details should support the family, guardian, dependant, or nominee details recorded; compare it with payment proof. |

4. After reconciling medical certificate, place dependent details with the computation and acknowledgement.
