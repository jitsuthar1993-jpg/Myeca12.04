# NPS Account Opening Checklist for Salaried and Self-Employed Users

NPS Account Opening Checklist for Salaried and Self-Employed Users starts with three concrete checks: whether PAN identifies the right applicant, Aadhaar or KYC support the claimed condition, and bank account fits the current instructions on NPS Trust official portal.

## National Pension System: confirm the live route and applicant fit

The first National Pension System decision is not whether every upload slot can be filled; it is whether the account holder, eligibility conditions, and payment instructions are supported, based on the current authority route and the facts shown by PAN, Aadhaar or KYC, and nominee details.

## Retirement savers: map PAN to nominee details

Bank account may expose a gap that PAN and Aadhaar or KYC do not settle, so retirement savers should preserve nominee details and obtain an accepted record or written clarification.

| Record | What it must support |
| --- | --- |
| PAN | PAN should answer the application question about applicant identity. Where it disagrees with Aadhaar or KYC, identify the authoritative record before uploading. |
| Aadhaar or KYC | Keep Aadhaar or KYC with its issuer and date visible. It should support applicant identity without contradicting the payment-account details shown by bank account. |
| bank account | Bank account is useful only when the application can trace payment-account details back to it. Resolve any incomplete or inconsistent NPS account opening documents 2026 detail before submission. |
| nominee details | Verify family, guardian, or nominee details from nominee details, then test the same applicant file against PAN. Save the checked NPS account opening documents 2026 evidence with the final acknowledgement. |

## Retirement savers: verify the authority route

The reference table gives retirement savers a source for each decision: discovery, current route, accepted PAN, and follow-up after the account request.

| Official reference | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| [myScheme - official government scheme discovery portal](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/) | Compare the discovery-page summary with bank account and nominee details; let the linked department instructions control the submission. |
| [NPS Trust official portal](https://www.npstrust.org.in/) | Compare the current portal instruction with Aadhaar or KYC and bank account; resolve a material conflict before the National Pension System request. |

## bank account conflict: decide before upload

When Aadhaar or KYC contradicts PAN, retirement savers should preserve both versions, ask the relevant authority for correction guidance, and use bank account only for payment-account details.

For National Pension System, compare the applicant name and identifier in PAN with Aadhaar or KYC. Correct the inaccurate issuing record through its own channel; use bank account only for payment-account details. Retain nominee details with the account-opening acknowledgement.

## nominee details case check: reconstruct the answer

Use one disputed applicant fact as the test case for National Pension System: identify the answer in PAN, compare Aadhaar or KYC, and explain why bank account supports or changes it.

Worked check for National Pension System: when PAN and Aadhaar or KYC identify the applicant differently, correct the inaccurate issuing record first. Use bank account only for payment-account details; retain nominee details with the account-opening acknowledgement.

## National Pension System account request: evidence limits and retention

Neither nominee details nor the account-opening acknowledgement proves that National Pension System has been finally accepted; keep both as evidence of the stage completed by retirement savers.

If bank account still leaves a material answer unsupported, retirement savers should pause the account request; otherwise retain nominee details and the account-opening acknowledgement together.
