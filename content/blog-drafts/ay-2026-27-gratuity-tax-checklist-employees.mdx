# AY 2026-27 Gratuity Tax Checklist for Employees

Gratuity statement and Form 16 can point to different filing answers for employees receiving gratuity; compare them before choosing a form or schedule, and use settlement letter to explain any material difference.

## Gratuity statement and Form 16: what each proves

Gratuity statement may establish one fact while Form 16 tests another. Keep gratuity statement, Form 16, the computation, and the acknowledgement distinct.

| Record | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| gratuity statement | Gratuity statement should show the amount, date, counterparty, and activity used in the filing working; compare it with bank credit proof. |
| Form 16 | Form 16 should support salary, perquisites, deductions, and employer TDS; compare it with gratuity statement. |
| settlement letter | Settlement letter should support the decision to match gratuity exemption, employer computation, Form 16, and final settlement documents; reconcile it with Form 16 before filing. |
| bank credit proof | Bank credit proof should show receipt or payment dates and amounts; compare them with settlement letter. |

## Gratuity statement and Form 16: decide the reporting treatment

Gratuity statement and Form 16 should explain the transaction total. Treat settlement letter as the cross-check for dates, amounts, counterparties, and classifications.

1. Use gratuity statement and Form 16 to settle the reporting treatment.
2. For employees receiving gratuity, record that comparison in the computation before selecting a form or schedule.
3. Use settlement letter to resolve the material difference before choosing a form.
4. After reconciling gratuity statement, place bank credit proof with the computation and acknowledgement.
