# PM Vishwakarma Registration and Document Checklist

PM Vishwakarma Registration and Document Checklist starts with three concrete checks: whether Aadhaar identifies the right applicant, bank account supports the claimed condition, and mobile number fits the current instructions on PM Vishwakarma official portal.

## PM Vishwakarma: confirm the live route and applicant fit

Before a PM Vishwakarma request, traditional artisans and craftspeople should write down the intended outcome, open [PM Vishwakarma official portal](https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/), and decide whether the identity, status, and registration details are supported from Aadhaar, bank account, and mobile number.

## trade details case check: reconstruct the answer

Treat trade details as part of the final trail, not proof of every earlier answer; the live instruction and the facts in Aadhaar, bank account, and mobile number still control the PM Vishwakarma request.

If mobile number is current but Aadhaar is not, identify whether the older record must be corrected or replaced. Retain trade details and the authority response with the final submission.

## Traditional artisans and craftspeople: map Aadhaar to trade details

Assign a distinct purpose to Aadhaar, bank account, and mobile number; note the issuer, relevant date, applicant detail, and application answer each record supports.

| Record | What it must support |
| --- | --- |
| Aadhaar | Keep Aadhaar with its issuer and date visible. It should support applicant identity without contradicting the payment-account details shown by bank account. |
| bank account | Bank account is useful only when the application can trace payment-account details back to it. Resolve any incomplete or inconsistent PM Vishwakarma registration documents 2026 detail before submission. |
| mobile number | Verify verification and alert details from mobile number, then test the same applicant file against trade details. Save the checked PM Vishwakarma registration documents 2026 evidence with the final acknowledgement. |
| trade details | Trade details should support the live application answer about trade, occupation, or business-activity details. For PM Vishwakarma registration documents 2026, use the format and issuing record named in the current authority instruction. |

## mobile number conflict: decide before upload

The PM Vishwakarma request should state one supported answer where Aadhaar and bank account overlap; unresolved differences belong in a correction request, not an inferred value from mobile number.

When Aadhaar and bank account point to different applicant facts, identify which authority owns the inaccurate record. Correct or clarify that record before using mobile number in the request.

## Traditional artisans and craftspeople: verify the authority route

PM Vishwakarma should be prepared from current official instructions, with Aadhaar and bank account checked against the source that actually controls the submission.

| Official reference | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| [myScheme - official government scheme discovery portal](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/) | Confirm the administering authority for PM Vishwakarma and its live department link before relying on the eligibility or document summary. |
| [PM Vishwakarma official portal](https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/) | Use this authority page to verify the live route for traditional artisans and craftspeople; record the check date beside bank account and mobile number. |

## PM Vishwakarma request: evidence limits and retention

Neither trade details nor the request acknowledgement proves that PM Vishwakarma has been finally accepted; keep both as evidence of the stage completed by traditional artisans and craftspeople.

The final PM Vishwakarma review should identify the intended outcome, authority route, uploaded Aadhaar, retained trade details, and owner of every remaining question.
