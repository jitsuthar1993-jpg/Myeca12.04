# AY 2026-27 High-Value Transaction AIS Checklist

A reliable return for taxpayers with high-value AIS entries should be rebuildable from AIS, transaction statements, and broker or bank records; if those records support different answers, pause before selecting the form or schedule.

## AIS and transaction statements: what each proves

Map amounts and dates to AIS, then test credits and classifications against transaction statements. Preserve the named-record trail through filing.

| Record | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| AIS | For high value transaction AIS AY 2026-27, read AIS as a third-party reporting signal, then investigate unsupported items against broker or bank records. |
| transaction statements | Transaction statements should show the amount, date, counterparty, and activity used in the filing working; compare it with property documents. |
| broker or bank records | Broker or bank records should show receipt or payment dates and amounts; compare them with AIS. |
| property documents | Property documents should establish the ownership share, property details, and relevant period; compare it with transaction statements. |

## AIS and transaction statements: decide the reporting treatment

The return should follow transactions supported by AIS and transaction statements. Check broker or bank records for taxpayers with high-value AIS entries before deciding the amount and income classification.

1. Use AIS and transaction statements to settle the reporting treatment.
2. For taxpayers with high-value AIS entries, record that comparison in the computation before selecting a form or schedule.
3. Let broker or bank records settle the open issue in the working before form selection.
4. After reconciling AIS, place property documents with the computation and acknowledgement.

## Taxpayers with high-value AIS entries: worked check using property documents

Compare a disputed AIS item with transaction statements, then use broker or bank records to settle its amount and classification. Keep property documents with the recorded conclusion.

## Taxpayers with high-value AIS entries: reconcile broker or bank records

Do not choose a transaction treatment from AIS alone. Compare it with transaction statements, resolve the difference through broker or bank records, and retain property documents with the recorded conclusion.

## AIS: final filing check

1. Taxpayers with high-value AIS entries should have one reporting treatment supported by the source records.
2. For taxpayers with high-value AIS entries, choose the form and schedules only after the material mismatch in broker or bank records is resolved in the computation.
3. For taxpayers with high-value AIS entries, retain property documents with the computation, submitted return, and acknowledgement.
