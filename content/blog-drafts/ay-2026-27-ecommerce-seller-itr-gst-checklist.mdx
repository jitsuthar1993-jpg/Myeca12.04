# AY 2026-27 Ecommerce Seller ITR and GST Checklist

Ecommerce sellers need a return that can be traced back to the records, not merely a plausible prefilled figure. Start with marketplace statement and GST returns, then use bank settlement report to settle the classification, credit, deduction, or disclosure at issue.

## Marketplace statement and GST returns: decide the reporting treatment

Rebuild the relevant transactions from marketplace statement and GST returns. Use bank settlement report to settle disagreements before choosing the form and classification.

1. Use marketplace statement and GST returns to settle the reporting treatment.
2. For ecommerce sellers, record that comparison in the computation before selecting a form or schedule.
3. Document how bank settlement report resolves the issue before choosing a form.
4. After reconciling marketplace statement, place expense records with the computation and acknowledgement.

## Marketplace statement and GST returns: what each proves

Assign a specific purpose to marketplace statement, GST returns, and every other return record. Do not let GST returns replace the fact established by marketplace statement.

| Record | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| marketplace statement | Marketplace statement should show the amount, date, counterparty, and activity used in the filing working; compare it with GST returns. |
| GST returns | GST returns should reconcile turnover and tax with invoices, books, bank receipts, and bank settlement report. |
| bank settlement report | Bank settlement report should show receipt or payment dates and amounts; compare them with expense records. |
| expense records | Expense records should show the amount, date, counterparty, and activity used in the filing working; compare it with marketplace statement. |

## Ecommerce sellers: reconcile bank settlement report

Trace a difference between marketplace statement and GST returns through bank settlement report. Check dates, amounts, counterparties, charges, and classifications; preserve expense records with the chosen treatment.

## Ecommerce sellers: worked check using expense records

Where marketplace statement and GST returns do not reconcile, identify the matching entry in bank settlement report. Record the reason for its treatment and retain expense records.
