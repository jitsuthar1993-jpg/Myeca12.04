# GeM Seller Registration Checklist for Small Businesses

Government e-Marketplace preparation is a fit-and-evidence decision for small businesses selling to government buyers; the work is to check business identity, bank, tax registration, product catalogue, and compliance records, compare PAN with bank account, and retain Udyam or GST if applicable and product details for follow-up.

## Small businesses selling to government buyers: map PAN to product details

For small businesses selling to government buyers, a reliable Government e-Marketplace file separates the job of PAN from the jobs of bank account, Udyam or GST if applicable, and product details, then records any missing or contradictory fact before upload.

| Record | What it must support |
| --- | --- |
| PAN | Keep PAN with its issuer and date visible. It should support applicant identity without contradicting the payment-account details shown by bank account. |
| bank account | Bank account is useful only when the application can trace payment-account details back to it. Resolve any incomplete or inconsistent GeM seller registration documents 2026 detail before submission. |
| Udyam or GST if applicable | Verify the specific fact it contains from Udyam or GST if applicable, then test the same applicant file against product details. Save the checked GeM seller registration documents 2026 evidence with the final acknowledgement. |
| product details | Product details should support the live application answer about trade, occupation, or business-activity details. For GeM seller registration documents 2026, use the format and issuing record named in the current authority instruction. |

## Government e-Marketplace: confirm the live route and applicant fit

Small businesses selling to government buyers should use PAN to establish the starting facts for Government e-Marketplace, then compare bank account and Udyam or GST if applicable with the current route shown by Government e-Marketplace official portal.

## Udyam or GST if applicable conflict: decide before upload

Compare PAN with bank account before small businesses selling to government buyers submit; if the records support different applicant facts, pause the request and keep Udyam or GST if applicable out of the final upload set.

When PAN and bank account point to different applicant facts, identify which authority owns the inaccurate record. Correct or clarify that record before using Udyam or GST if applicable in the request.

## Small businesses selling to government buyers: verify the authority route

Government e-Marketplace should be prepared from current official instructions, with PAN and bank account checked against the source that actually controls the submission.

| Official reference | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| [myScheme - official government scheme discovery portal](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/) | Use the listing to find the official application owner for small businesses selling to government buyers, then verify the current process and accepted evidence on that authority's page. |
| [Government e-Marketplace official portal](https://gem.gov.in/) | Check the live instructions for bank account; save the page title, URL, date checked, and any unresolved Udyam or GST if applicable question. |
| [Udyam Registration official portal](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/) | Use this page to confirm the deciding authority and required evidence before small businesses selling to government buyers rely on Udyam or GST if applicable. |

## product details case check: reconstruct the answer

Before the request, compare the proposed answer with PAN and bank account; use Udyam or GST if applicable only for its stated purpose and retain product details with the conclusion.

If Udyam or GST if applicable is current but PAN is not, identify whether the older record must be corrected or replaced. Retain product details and the authority response with the final submission.

## Government e-Marketplace request: evidence limits and retention

The Government e-Marketplace request should stop when PAN or bank account supports a contradictory material answer; a later submission is preferable to an inaccurate or unreconstructable file.

Keep one Government e-Marketplace request folder containing the checked authority page, PAN, bank account, product details, the request acknowledgement, and later communication.
