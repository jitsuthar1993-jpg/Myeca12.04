# Caste Certificate Checklist for Scholarship and Loan Schemes

Caste certificate preparation is a fit-and-evidence decision for applicants using category-based schemes; the work is to check certificate validity, name consistency, family records, and portal upload requirements, compare issued category certificate with Aadhaar, and retain family records and education or business proof for follow-up.

## Caste certificate: confirm the live route and applicant fit

Caste certificate preparation should pause until applicants using category-based schemes can connect the live instruction to issued category certificate, explain the role of Aadhaar, and record whether the student, institution, income, and academic conditions are supported without guessing.

## family records conflict: decide before upload

Treat a missing issued category certificate differently from a contradictory Aadhaar: the first may need an accepted alternative, while the second needs correction or authority guidance before uploading family records.

Check whether issued category certificate and Aadhaar describe the same applicant, period, and activity. If not, pause the application; use family records only for family, guardian, or nominee details.

## Applicants using category-based schemes: map issued category certificate to education or business proof

Applicants using category-based schemes should read issued category certificate, Aadhaar, family records, and education or business proof as a connected record set, but preserve the separate purpose and issuing source of each item.

| Record | What it must support |
| --- | --- |
| issued category certificate | Issued category certificate should support the live application answer about eligibility status. For caste certificate scholarship loan scheme 2026, use the format and issuing record named in the current authority instruction. |
| Aadhaar | Aadhaar should support applicant identity exactly as entered. Compare any different name, date, identifier, or status with family records before submission. |
| family records | Use family records for family, guardian, or nominee details; use education or business proof separately for education, institution, course, or training details. Resolve any caste certificate scholarship loan scheme 2026 conflict through source-record correction or written clarification. |
| education or business proof | Education or business proof belongs in the file because it supports education, institution, course, or training details. Confirm the issuer, validity, applicant details, and relevant period before relying on education or business proof. |

## education or business proof case check: reconstruct the answer

A later verification request should be answerable from the file itself, so use issued category certificate to establish the starting fact, family records to explain any change, and education or business proof to preserve the outcome.

Test the application answer against issued category certificate, Aadhaar, and family records. If family records support a different applicant fact, preserve the issue and request clarification before submission.
