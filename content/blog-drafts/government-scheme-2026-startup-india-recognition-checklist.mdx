# Startup India Recognition Checklist for Founders in 2026

Startup India Recognition Checklist for Founders in 2026 starts with three concrete checks: whether incorporation certificate identifies the right applicant, PAN supports the claimed condition, and pitch or innovation note fit the current instructions on Startup India official portal.

## Startup India recognition: confirm the live route and applicant fit

Startup India recognition preparation should pause until startup founders can connect the live instruction to incorporation certificate, explain the role of PAN, and record whether the applicant status and recognition conditions are supported without guessing.

## pitch or innovation note conflict: decide before upload

When PAN contradicts incorporation certificate, startup founders should preserve both versions, ask the relevant authority for correction guidance, and use pitch or innovation note only for activity, cost, or funding details.

If pitch or innovation note exposes a difference between incorporation certificate and PAN, document the issue and responsible issuing authority. Submit only after the evidence supports one answer.

## Startup founders: map incorporation certificate to authorisation proof

incorporation certificate, PAN, and pitch or innovation note each support a different part of Startup India recognition documents 2026; do not use one record as a substitute for another.

| Record | What it must support |
| --- | --- |
| incorporation certificate | Incorporation certificate should be current, legible, and linked to the same applicant as PAN. Retain the version used for the submission. |
| PAN | Record what PAN proves about applicant identity, then compare that answer with pitch or innovation note. For Startup India recognition documents 2026, use each record only for the fact it actually contains. |
| pitch or innovation note | Pitch or innovation note should answer the application question about activity, cost, or funding details. Where it disagrees with authorisation proof, identify the authoritative record before uploading. |
| authorisation proof | Keep authorisation proof with its issuer and date visible. It should support the specific fact it contains without contradicting the household, residence, or address details shown by incorporation certificate. |

## authorisation proof case check: reconstruct the answer

Authorisation proof should make the completed request easier to reconstruct; if incorporation certificate and PAN still conflict, the file is not ready for that step.

A material fact missing from incorporation certificate and PAN should pause the request. Confirm an accepted record, use pitch or innovation note only for activity, cost, or funding details, and retain authorisation proof.

## Startup founders: verify the authority route

PAN may be accepted differently across routes or dates, so startup founders should retain the authority page, URL, and check date used for the request.

| Official reference | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| [myScheme - official government scheme discovery portal](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/) | Use the listing to identify the department responsible for Startup India recognition; confirm its accepted-record summary against pitch or innovation note and authorisation proof. |
| [Startup India official portal](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) | Compare the current portal instruction with PAN and pitch or innovation note; resolve a material conflict before the Startup India recognition request. |

## Startup India recognition: evidence limits and retention

Location, applicant facts, and submission date may change the Startup India recognition route for startup founders; unresolved ownership, legal, tax, or eligibility questions need authority guidance or suitable professional advice.

Startup founders should not submit merely because every upload slot is filled; the facts in incorporation certificate, PAN, and pitch or innovation note must support the same request.
