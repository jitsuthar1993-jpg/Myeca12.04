# PMSBY Accident Insurance Checklist for Workers and Families

A PMSBY claim or coverage request can contain every requested upload and still be unreliable when bank account, nominee details, premium debit proof, and identity proof describe different facts. Workers seeking accident cover should resolve those differences against Pradhan Mantri Jan-Dhan Yojana official portal before they submit.

## PMSBY: confirm the live route and applicant fit

Before a PMSBY claim or coverage request, workers seeking accident cover should write down the intended outcome, open [Pradhan Mantri Jan-Dhan Yojana official portal](https://pmjdy.gov.in/), and decide whether the event, coverage, applicant, and timing conditions are supported from bank account, nominee details, and premium debit proof.

## identity proof case check: reconstruct the answer

Test the PMSBY claim or coverage request before portal entry: ask which live instruction is answered by bank account, which fact is confirmed by nominee details, and why identity proof belongs with the result.

Before the final upload of identity proof, ask which single record establishes the disputed fact. Use that answer to correct the claim or coverage request, then retain identity proof with the claim or coverage acknowledgement.

## Workers seeking accident cover: map bank account to identity proof

Workers seeking accident cover should read bank account, nominee details, premium debit proof, and identity proof as a connected record set, but preserve the separate purpose and issuing source of each item.

| Record | What it must support |
| --- | --- |
| bank account | Bank account should support payment-account details exactly as entered. Compare any different name, date, identifier, or status with nominee details before submission. |
| nominee details | Use nominee details for family, guardian, or nominee details; use premium debit proof separately for coverage, premium, event, or claim details. Resolve any PMSBY accident insurance checklist 2026 conflict through source-record correction or written clarification. |
| premium debit proof | Premium debit proof belongs in the file because it supports coverage, premium, event, or claim details. Confirm the issuer, validity, applicant details, and relevant period before relying on premium debit proof. |
| identity proof | Read identity proof only for applicant identity. If bank account points to a different applicant fact, pause the request and resolve that difference first. |

## premium debit proof conflict: decide before upload

Use premium debit proof only for the fact it proves. If bank account and nominee details conflict, correct the issuing record or obtain written guidance before submission.

For a PMSBY claim or coverage request, check whether bank account and nominee details describe the same applicant, event, period, and account. Explain a material difference with premium debit proof, and retain identity proof with the claim or coverage acknowledgement.

## Workers seeking accident cover: verify the authority route

Workers seeking accident cover should separate scheme discovery from the final authority instruction: locate PMSBY, verify the owner of the route, and retain the source used for bank account.

| Official reference | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| [myScheme - official government scheme discovery portal](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/) | Confirm the administering authority for PMSBY and its live department link before relying on the eligibility or document summary. |
| [Pradhan Mantri Jan-Dhan Yojana official portal](https://pmjdy.gov.in/) | Read the current instructions before upload, then compare the required nominee details with premium debit proof in the PMSBY file. |

## PMSBY claim or coverage request: evidence limits and retention

PMSBY approval and processing remain decisions of Pradhan Mantri Jan-Dhan Yojana official portal; a consistent file for workers seeking accident cover reduces avoidable questions but does not guarantee the outcome.

Workers seeking accident cover can move to the next step when the live PMSBY route is confirmed, bank account and nominee details support the material answers, and identity proof is ready for the follow-up trail.
