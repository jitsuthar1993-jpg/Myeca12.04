# PM-KISAN Eligibility and Tax Record Checklist for 2026

PM-KISAN Eligibility and Tax Record Checklist for 2026 starts with three concrete checks: whether Aadhaar identifies the right applicant, land record supports the claimed condition, and bank passbook fits the current instructions on PM-KISAN official portal.

## PM-KISAN: confirm the live route and applicant fit

Before a PM-KISAN application, small and marginal farmers should write down the intended outcome, open [PM-KISAN official portal](https://pmkisan.gov.in/), and decide whether the applicant and every material condition are supported from Aadhaar, land record, and bank passbook.

## mobile number case check: reconstruct the answer

The strongest pre-submission question for small and marginal farmers is practical: if the authority asks about bank passbook, can the answer be traced back to Aadhaar or land record?

Having Aadhaar, land record, and bank passbook ready does not resolve a contradiction among them. The unresolved PM KISAN eligibility 2026 fact should name the issuing record, correction owner, and evidence required before submission.

## Small and marginal farmers: map Aadhaar to mobile number

The PM-KISAN application record map begins with Aadhaar and ends with mobile number; land record and bank passbook should resolve separate questions rather than repeat the same unsupported answer.

| Record | What it must support |
| --- | --- |
| Aadhaar | Aadhaar is useful only when the application can trace applicant identity back to it. Resolve any incomplete or inconsistent PM KISAN eligibility 2026 detail before submission. |
| land record | Verify land, crop, or activity details from land record, then test the same applicant file against bank passbook. Save the checked PM KISAN eligibility 2026 evidence with the final acknowledgement. |
| bank passbook | Bank passbook should support the live application answer about payment-account details. For PM KISAN eligibility 2026, use the format and issuing record named in the current authority instruction. |
| mobile number | Mobile number should support verification and alert details exactly as entered. Compare any different name, date, identifier, or status with Aadhaar before submission. |

## bank passbook conflict: decide before upload

When land record contradicts Aadhaar, small and marginal farmers should preserve both versions, ask the relevant authority for correction guidance, and use bank passbook only for payment-account details.

Do not use land record to fill a gap that only Aadhaar can answer. Resolve the difference, record what bank passbook proves, and retain mobile number used for alerts and verification.

## Small and marginal farmers: verify the authority route

Record the official page used for PM-KISAN, the date it was checked, and the unresolved question involving bank passbook; keep that note with mobile number.

| Official reference | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| [myScheme - official government scheme discovery portal](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/) | Confirm the administering authority for PM-KISAN and its live department link before relying on the eligibility or document summary. |
| [PM-KISAN official portal](https://pmkisan.gov.in/) | Compare the current portal instruction with land record and bank passbook; resolve a material conflict before the PM-KISAN request. |

## PM-KISAN application: evidence limits and retention

PM-KISAN remains subject to current authority checks even after the application; preserve Aadhaar, mobile number, and later clarification requests until the matter is closed.

The next action for small and marginal farmers should follow the evidence: correct Aadhaar, clarify land record, use bank passbook, or proceed and retain mobile number.
