## Sales summary and bank statement: decide the reporting treatment

The return should follow transactions supported by sales summary and bank statement. Check GST records if any for small shop owners before deciding the amount and income classification.

1. Use sales summary and bank statement to settle the reporting treatment.
2. For small shop owners, record that comparison in the computation before selecting a form or schedule.
