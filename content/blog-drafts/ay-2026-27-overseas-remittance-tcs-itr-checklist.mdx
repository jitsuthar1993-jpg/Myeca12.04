# AY 2026-27 Overseas Remittance TCS ITR Checklist

Bank remittance advice and TCS certificate can point to different filing answers for users making overseas remittances; compare them before choosing a form or schedule, and use Form 26AS to explain any material difference.

## Bank remittance advice and TCS certificate: decide the reporting treatment

Compare the payer and gross receipt in bank remittance advice with the period and credit shown by TCS certificate. Use Form 26AS to resolve the conflict, and claim only the credit supported by those records.

1. Use bank remittance advice and TCS certificate to settle the reporting treatment.
2. For users making overseas remittances, record that comparison in the computation before selecting a form or schedule.
3. Use Form 26AS to resolve the material difference before choosing a form.
4. After reconciling bank remittance advice, place AIS with the computation and acknowledgement.

## Bank remittance advice and TCS certificate: what each proves

Bank remittance advice may establish one fact while TCS certificate tests another. Keep bank remittance advice, TCS certificate, the computation, and the acknowledgement distinct.

| Record | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| bank remittance advice | Bank remittance advice should show receipt or payment dates and amounts; compare them with TCS certificate. |
| TCS certificate | Trace the credit to the payer record, related receipt, AIS, Form 26AS, and Form 26AS before claiming it. |
| Form 26AS | Form 26AS should confirm TDS, TCS, and tax payments claimed; compare a missing or incorrect credit with AIS. |
| AIS | For overseas remittance TCS ITR AY 2026-27, read AIS as a third-party reporting signal, then investigate unsupported items against bank remittance advice. |

## Users making overseas remittances: reconcile Form 26AS

Compare the payer and period in bank remittance advice with the receipt and deduction in TCS certificate. A difference can change the credit claim; use Form 26AS to identify it and retain AIS with the correction trail.

## Users making overseas remittances: worked check using AIS

Bank remittance advice and TCS certificate may agree on a receipt while showing different credit. Identify the relevant payer and deduction in Form 26AS; claim the credit only after retaining AIS with its correction trail.
