Treat a missing policy acknowledgement differently from a contradictory crop details: the first may need an accepted alternative, while the second needs correction or authority guidance before uploading bank account.

For a PM Fasal Bima Yojana claim or coverage request, check whether policy acknowledgement and crop details describe the same applicant, event, period, and account. Explain a material difference with bank account, and retain loss record with the claim or coverage acknowledgement.
