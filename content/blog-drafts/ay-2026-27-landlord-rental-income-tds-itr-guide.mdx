# AY 2026-27 Landlord Rental Income and TDS ITR Guide

Rent agreement and tenant TDS certificate can point to different filing answers for landlords earning rent; compare them before choosing a form or schedule, and use home loan certificate to explain any material difference.

## Rent agreement and tenant TDS certificate: what each proves

Rent agreement may establish one fact while tenant TDS certificate tests another. Keep rent agreement, tenant TDS certificate, the computation, and the acknowledgement distinct.

| Record | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| rent agreement | Rent agreement should show the property, rental period, parties, and payment; compare it with tenant TDS certificate. |
| tenant TDS certificate | Trace the credit to the payer record, related receipt, AIS, Form 26AS, and home loan certificate before claiming it. |
| home loan certificate | Home loan certificate should establish the amount, period, taxpayer fact, and issuer used in the working; compare it with municipal tax proof. |
| municipal tax proof | Municipal tax proof should show the amount, date, counterparty, and activity used in the filing working; compare it with rent agreement. |

## Rent agreement and tenant TDS certificate: decide the reporting treatment

Compare the payer and gross receipt in rent agreement with the period and credit shown by tenant TDS certificate. Use home loan certificate to resolve the conflict, and claim only the credit supported by those records.

1. Use rent agreement and tenant TDS certificate to settle the reporting treatment.
2. For landlords earning rent, record that comparison in the computation before selecting a form or schedule.
3. Use home loan certificate to resolve the material difference before choosing a form.
4. After reconciling rent agreement, place municipal tax proof with the computation and acknowledgement.

## Landlords earning rent: worked check using municipal tax proof

Rent agreement and tenant TDS certificate may agree on a receipt while showing different credit. Identify the relevant payer and deduction in home loan certificate; claim the credit only after retaining municipal tax proof with its correction trail.

## Landlords earning rent: reconcile home loan certificate

Compare the payer and period in rent agreement with the receipt and deduction in tenant TDS certificate. A difference can change the credit claim; use home loan certificate to identify it and retain municipal tax proof with the correction trail.
