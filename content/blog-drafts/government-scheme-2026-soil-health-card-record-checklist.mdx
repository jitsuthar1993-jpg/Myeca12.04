# Soil Health Card Record Checklist for Farmers

Soil Health Card preparation is a fit-and-evidence decision for farmers checking soil records; the work is to keep soil-test, land, crop, and advisory records for better farm planning, compare land record with soil sample details, and retain crop details and mobile number for follow-up.

## Soil Health Card: confirm the live route and applicant fit

The first Soil Health Card decision is not whether every upload slot can be filled; it is whether the identity, status, and requested service are supported, based on the current authority route and the facts shown by land record, soil sample details, and mobile number.

## Farmers checking soil records: map land record to mobile number

Assign a distinct purpose to land record, soil sample details, and crop details; note the issuer, relevant date, applicant detail, and application answer each record supports.

| Record | What it must support |
| --- | --- |
| land record | Use land record for land, crop, or activity details; use soil sample details separately for land, crop, or activity details. Resolve any Soil Health Card 2026 conflict through source-record correction or written clarification. |
| soil sample details | Soil sample details belong in the file because they support land, crop, or activity details. Confirm the issuer, validity, applicant details, and relevant period before relying on soil sample details. |
| crop details | Read crop details only for land, crop, or activity details. If mobile number points to a different applicant fact, pause the request and resolve that difference first. |
| mobile number | Mobile number should be current, legible, and linked to the same applicant as land record. Retain the version used for the submission. |

## Farmers checking soil records: verify the authority route

Before the Soil Health Card service request, check the current applicant category, accepted format for land record, treatment of soil sample details, and follow-up channel shown by the official references.

| Official reference | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| [myScheme - official government scheme discovery portal](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/) | Compare the discovery-page summary with crop details and mobile number; let the linked department instructions control the submission. |
| [Soil Health Card official portal](https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/) | Confirm the current category for farmers checking soil records, accepted soil sample details, application channel, and any live deadline; compare those instructions with crop details. |

## crop details conflict: decide before upload

Treat a missing land record differently from a contradictory soil sample details: the first may need an accepted alternative, while the second needs correction or authority guidance before uploading crop details.

Separate the question answered by land record from the one answered by soil sample details. Resolve a material inconsistency before uploading crop details.

## mobile number case check: reconstruct the answer

Before farmers checking soil records submit, recreate the path from the current Soil Health Card instruction to the value entered, the role of soil sample details, and the retained mobile number.

If the live instructions require a fact not shown by land record or soil sample details, obtain the accepted evidence instead of substituting crop details. Keep mobile number in the follow-up file.

## Soil Health Card service request: evidence limits and retention

The useful limit of this guide is document readiness for farmers checking soil records: it explains how to review land record and soil sample details, not how the authority must decide Soil Health Card.

Use the checked Soil Health Card official portal route for the service request, then keep land record, mobile number, and the request acknowledgement in the same follow-up file.
