# AY 2026-27 ITR Guide for First-Time Salaried Employees

A reliable return for first-time salaried employees should be rebuildable from PAN and Aadhaar records, Form 16, and AIS and Form 26AS; if those records support different answers, pause before selecting the form or schedule.

## PAN and Aadhaar records and Form 16: decide the reporting treatment

First confirm that PAN and Aadhaar records and Form 16 identify the same taxpayer and period. After that identity check, use AIS and Form 26AS to verify the payer, receipt, and credit reported for first-time salaried employees and retain bank interest certificate with the computation.

1. Use PAN and Aadhaar records and Form 16 to settle the reporting treatment.
2. For first-time salaried employees, record that comparison in the computation before selecting a form or schedule.
3. Let AIS and Form 26AS settle the open issue in the working before form selection.
4. After reconciling PAN and Aadhaar records, place bank interest certificate with the computation and acknowledgement.

## PAN and Aadhaar records and Form 16: what each proves

Map amounts and dates to PAN and Aadhaar records, then test credits and classifications against Form 16. Preserve the named-record trail through filing.

| Record | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| PAN and Aadhaar records | PAN and Aadhaar records should match the taxpayer identity and profile details used in the filing working; compare it with Form 16. |
| Form 16 | Form 16 should support salary, perquisites, deductions, and employer TDS; compare it with AIS and Form 26AS. |
| AIS and Form 26AS | Use AIS and Form 26AS to identify third-party-reported entries, then investigate unsupported items against bank interest certificate. |
| bank interest certificate | Bank interest certificate should show receipt or payment dates and amounts; compare them with PAN and Aadhaar records. |

## First-time salaried employees: reconcile AIS and Form 26AS

A different name or identifier in PAN and Aadhaar records and Form 16 is an identity problem, not a filing adjustment. Correct the inaccurate issuing record; use AIS and Form 26AS only to verify the payer, receipt, and credit reported for first-time salaried employees, and retain the correction acknowledgement with bank interest certificate.

## First-time salaried employees: worked check using bank interest certificate

If PAN and Aadhaar records identify the taxpayer differently from Form 16, decide which issuing record is inaccurate. Correct it before filing; use AIS and Form 26AS only to verify the payer, receipt, and credit reported for first-time salaried employees, and retain the acknowledgement with bank interest certificate.

## Choosing the assessment year, Form 16, bank interest, and e-verification without rushing the return: limits and pause points

The proposed treatment for first-time salaried employees must fit the complete taxpayer position before it can be used.

## PAN and Aadhaar records: final filing check

1. First-time salaried employees should have one reporting treatment supported by the source records.
2. For first-time salaried employees, choose the form and schedules only after the material mismatch in AIS and Form 26AS is resolved in the computation.
3. For first-time salaried employees, retain bank interest certificate with the computation, submitted return, and acknowledgement.
