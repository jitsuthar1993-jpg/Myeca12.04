## Form 16A and client ledger: what each proves

Map amounts and dates to Form 16A, then test credits and classifications against client ledger. Preserve the named-record trail through filing.

| Record | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Form 16A | Match the payer, gross receipt, and deducted tax with AIS, Form 26AS, and professional expense list before claiming credit. |
| client ledger | Client ledger should show the amount, date, counterparty, and activity used in the filing working; compare it with Form 16A. |
| bank statement | Bank statement should show receipt or payment dates and amounts; compare them with client ledger. |
| professional expense list | Professional expense list should support the decision to match professional fees, TDS, expenses, and form selection before claiming refunds; reconcile it with bank statement before filing. |

## Form 16A and client ledger: decide the reporting treatment

The return should follow transactions supported by Form 16A and client ledger. Check bank statement for lawyers and legal consultants before deciding the amount and income classification.

3. Let bank statement settle the open issue in the working before form selection.
4. After reconciling Form 16A, place professional expense list with the computation and acknowledgement.

## Lawyers and legal consultants: reconcile bank statement
