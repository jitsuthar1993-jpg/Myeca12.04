# AY 2026-27 ITR Guide for Pensioners and Family Pension

Pension certificate and Form 16 from pension payer can point to different filing answers for pensioners and family-pension recipients; compare them before choosing a form or schedule, and use bank interest certificate to explain any material difference.

## Pension certificate and Form 16 from pension payer: decide the reporting treatment

Pension certificate should establish the claimed amount or status, while Form 16 from pension payer tests a separate condition. Before deciding the return treatment, check bank interest certificate for a conflicting taxpayer fact.

1. Use pension certificate and Form 16 from pension payer to settle the reporting treatment.
2. For pensioners and family-pension recipients, record that comparison in the computation before selecting a form or schedule.
3. Use bank interest certificate to resolve the material difference before choosing a form.
4. After reconciling pension certificate, place Form 26AS with the computation and acknowledgement.

## Pensioners and family-pension recipients: reconcile bank interest certificate

For pensioners and family-pension recipients, resolve any conflict between the claim supported by pension certificate and the condition shown by Form 16 from pension payer. Use bank interest certificate to test the disputed condition, then retain Form 26AS with the chosen treatment.

## Pension certificate and Form 16 from pension payer: what each proves

Pension certificate may establish one fact while Form 16 from pension payer tests another. Keep pension certificate, Form 16 from pension payer, the computation, and the acknowledgement distinct.

| Record | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| pension certificate | Pension certificate should establish the amount, period, taxpayer fact, and issuer used in the working; compare it with Form 26AS. |
| Form 16 from pension payer | Form 16 from pension payer should support salary, perquisites, deductions, and employer TDS; compare it with pension certificate. |
| bank interest certificate | Bank interest certificate should show receipt or payment dates and amounts; compare them with Form 16 from pension payer. |
| Form 26AS | Form 26AS should confirm TDS, TCS, and tax payments claimed; compare a missing or incorrect credit with bank interest certificate. |

## Pensioners and family-pension recipients: worked check using Form 26AS

If pension certificate supports a claim that Form 16 from pension payer does not, use bank interest certificate to identify the unsupported condition. Retain Form 26AS with the reason for the chosen treatment.

## Reporting pension, family pension, bank interest, deductions, and refund details conservatively: limits and pause points

Bank interest certificate leaves the return incomplete when a credit, deduction, classification, or disclosure remains unresolved for pensioners and family-pension recipients.

## Pension certificate: final filing check

1. Pensioners and family-pension recipients should have one reporting treatment supported by the source records.
2. For pensioners and family-pension recipients, choose the form and schedules only after the material mismatch in bank interest certificate is resolved in the computation.
3. For pensioners and family-pension recipients, retain Form 26AS with the computation, submitted return, and acknowledgement.
