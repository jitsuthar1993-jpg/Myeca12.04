# MSME Samadhaan Delayed Payment Complaint Checklist

A MSME Samadhaan complaint can contain every requested upload and still be unreliable when Udyam certificate, invoice, purchase order, and delivery proof describe different facts. MSMEs facing delayed payments should resolve those differences against MSME Samadhaan official portal before they file.

## MSME Samadhaan: confirm the live route and applicant fit

The first MSME Samadhaan decision is not whether every upload slot can be filled; it is whether the delayed-payment transaction and supplier status are supported, based on the current authority route and the facts shown by Udyam certificate, invoice, and delivery proof.

## MSMEs facing delayed payments: map Udyam certificate to delivery proof

Invoice should not be used as a substitute for Udyam certificate; assign purchase order and delivery proof their own application questions so the MSME Samadhaan complaint can be reconstructed later.

| Record | What it must support |
| --- | --- |
| Udyam certificate | Udyam certificate is useful only when the application can trace eligibility status back to it. Resolve any incomplete or inconsistent MSME Samadhaan complaint documents 2026 detail before submission. |
| invoice | Verify transaction or activity details from invoice, then test the same applicant file against purchase order. Save the checked MSME Samadhaan complaint documents 2026 evidence with the final acknowledgement. |
| purchase order | Purchase order should support the live application answer about transaction or activity details. For MSME Samadhaan complaint documents 2026, use the format and issuing record named in the current authority instruction. |
| delivery proof | Delivery proof should support transaction or activity details exactly as entered. Compare any different name, date, identifier, or status with Udyam certificate before submission. |

## MSMEs facing delayed payments: verify the authority route

Record the official page used for MSME Samadhaan, the date it was checked, and the unresolved question involving purchase order; keep that note with delivery proof.

| Official reference | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| [myScheme - official government scheme discovery portal](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/) | Compare the discovery-page summary with purchase order and delivery proof; let the linked department instructions control the submission. |
| [MSME Samadhaan official portal](https://samadhaan.msme.gov.in/) | Use this page to confirm the deciding authority and required evidence before MSMEs facing delayed payments rely on invoice. |
| [Udyam Registration official portal](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/) | Compare the current portal instruction with purchase order and delivery proof; resolve a material conflict before the MSME Samadhaan request. |

## purchase order conflict: decide before upload

MSME Samadhaan should proceed only after Udyam certificate and invoice support one answer for the disputed fact; purchase order can add evidence but cannot repair an inaccurate issuing record.

Before filing a complaint through MSME Samadhaan, connect the invoice, purchase order, delivery or acceptance evidence, and payment history. Explain the difference between Udyam certificate and invoice with purchase order; keep delivery proof and the complaint acknowledgement in that transaction file.

## delivery proof case check: reconstruct the answer

Test the MSME Samadhaan complaint before portal entry: ask which live instruction is answered by Udyam certificate, which fact is confirmed by invoice, and why delivery proof belongs with the result.

Having Udyam certificate, invoice, and purchase order ready does not resolve a contradiction among them. The unresolved MSME Samadhaan complaint documents 2026 fact should name the issuing record, correction owner, and evidence required before submission.

## MSME Samadhaan complaint: evidence limits and retention

MSME Samadhaan may require correction or grievance steps after submission, so MSMEs facing delayed payments should preserve purchase order, delivery proof, and every message received from the authority.

MSMEs facing delayed payments should not file merely because every upload slot is filled; the facts in Udyam certificate, invoice, and purchase order must support the same request.
