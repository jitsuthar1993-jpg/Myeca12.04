# Disability Certificate Checklist for Tax and Scheme Benefits

A disability certificate service request can contain every requested upload and still be unreliable when issued certificate, medical records, Aadhaar, and bank account describe different facts. Persons with disabilities and caregivers should resolve those differences against myScheme - official government scheme discovery portal before they submit.

## Persons with disabilities and caregivers: map issued certificate to bank account

Before converting files for disability certificate, note the issuer, relevant date, applicant details, and supported answer for issued certificate, medical records, Aadhaar, and bank account.

| Record | What it must support |
| --- | --- |
| issued certificate | Issued certificate should support eligibility status exactly as entered. Compare any different name, date, identifier, or status with medical records before submission. |
| medical records | Use medical records for the specific fact it contains; use Aadhaar separately for applicant identity. Resolve any disability certificate tax scheme 2026 conflict through source-record correction or written clarification. |
| Aadhaar | Aadhaar belongs in the file because it supports applicant identity. Confirm the issuer, validity, applicant details, and relevant period before relying on Aadhaar. |
| bank account | Read bank account only for payment-account details. If issued certificate points to a different applicant fact, pause the request and resolve that difference first. |

## bank account case check: reconstruct the answer

Before persons with disabilities and caregivers submit, recreate the path from the current disability certificate instruction to the value entered, the role of medical records, and the retained bank account.

Before the final upload of bank account, ask which single record establishes the disputed fact. Use that answer to correct the service request, then retain bank account with the request acknowledgement.

## Disability certificate: confirm the live route and applicant fit

Use myScheme - official government scheme discovery portal to confirm the current application channel and accepted records, then decide whether disability certificate and medical records support the requested disability certificate tax scheme 2026 outcome.

## Aadhaar conflict: decide before upload

Bank account belongs in the follow-up trail when issued certificate, medical records, or Aadhaar changes the route or applicant answer used for disability certificate.

Where issued certificate is incomplete, do not infer the missing answer from medical records. Obtain an accepted record or written clarification, then preserve bank account.

## Persons with disabilities and caregivers: verify the authority route

Persons with disabilities and caregivers should separate scheme discovery from the final authority instruction: locate disability certificate, verify the owner of the route, and retain the source used for issued certificate.

| Official reference | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| [myScheme - official government scheme discovery portal](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/) | Check whether the listing still points persons with disabilities and caregivers to the current authority route, then compare its document summary with Aadhaar. |

## disability certificate service request: evidence limits and retention

Persons with disabilities and caregivers should not create a second inconsistent disability certificate service request to bypass a record problem; use the correction or grievance route linked by myScheme - official government scheme discovery portal.

The final disability certificate review should identify the intended outcome, authority route, uploaded issued certificate, retained bank account, and owner of every remaining question.
