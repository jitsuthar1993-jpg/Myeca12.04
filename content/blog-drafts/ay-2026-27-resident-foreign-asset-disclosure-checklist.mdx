# AY 2026-27 Foreign Asset Disclosure Checklist for Residents

Resident taxpayers with foreign assets need a return that can be traced back to the records, not merely a plausible prefilled figure. Start with foreign account statement and broker report, then use Form 67 records to settle the classification, credit, deduction, or disclosure at issue.

## Resident taxpayers with foreign assets: worked check using resident status working

Where foreign account statement and broker report do not reconcile, identify the matching entry in Form 67 records. Record the reason for its treatment and retain resident status working.

## Foreign account statement and broker report: decide the reporting treatment

Rebuild the relevant transactions from foreign account statement and broker report. Use Form 67 records to settle disagreements before choosing the form and classification.

1. Use foreign account statement and broker report to settle the reporting treatment.
2. For resident taxpayers with foreign assets, record that comparison in the computation before selecting a form or schedule.
3. Document how Form 67 records resolves the issue before choosing a form.
4. After reconciling foreign account statement, place resident status working with the computation and acknowledgement.

## Foreign account statement and broker report: what each proves

Assign a specific purpose to foreign account statement, broker report, and every other return record. Do not let broker report replace the fact established by foreign account statement.

| Record | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| foreign account statement | Foreign account statement should show the amount, date, counterparty, and activity used in the filing working; compare it with resident status working. |
| broker report | Broker report should establish transaction dates, values, charges, and the resulting gain, loss, or holding-period treatment; compare it with foreign account statement. |
| Form 67 records | Form 67 records should show the amount, date, counterparty, and activity used in the filing working; compare it with broker report. |
| resident status working | Resident status working should support the decision to check Schedule FA, foreign income, tax credit, and records for overseas accounts or shares; reconcile it with Form 67 records before filing. |

## Resident taxpayers with foreign assets: reconcile Form 67 records

Trace a difference between foreign account statement and broker report through Form 67 records. Check dates, amounts, counterparties, charges, and classifications; preserve resident status working with the chosen treatment.
