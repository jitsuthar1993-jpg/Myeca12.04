# ITR Season 2026 Paid Scale Gates

## Pilot Release

- Release an initial INR 25,000 pilot focused on Google Search.
- Route conversion-focused search traffic to `/which-itr-form-to-file?source=paid_search`.
- Treat INR 200,000 per month as a ceiling, not an automatic commitment.

## Required Reconciliation

Do not scale beyond the pilot until 30 reconciled payments match across the ad platform, analytics, payment records, and backend records.

All gates must pass:

- At least 95% attribution accuracy.
- Paid CPA below allowable CPA.
- Refund and cancellation rate below 10%.
- Available seven-day fulfillment capacity at least 1.5x forecast demand.
- SLA breach rate below 5%.

Allowable CPA equals net collected revenue minus GST, payment fees, incentives, fulfillment cost, and a 20% contribution-margin reserve.

## Stop Rules

Pause or reduce paid spend when attribution breaks, capacity coverage falls below forecast, SLA breaches reach 5%, or paid CPA exceeds allowable CPA.

LinkedIn and YouTube acquisition tests remain paused until at least 100 paid clients have been measured.
