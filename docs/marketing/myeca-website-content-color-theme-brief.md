# MyeCA.in Website, Content, Color, and Theme Brief

This brief is the source of truth for content, marketing, and public-page copy decisions on MyeCA.in. It turns the site positioning into practical rules for homepage copy, service pages, blogs, calculators, trust content, and campaign assets.

## 1. Strategic Positioning

MyeCA.in should feel like a calm, expert-led tax filing and compliance platform for Indian taxpayers, freelancers, investors, startups, and small businesses.

The operating promise is simple:

> clear scope before payment, secure document handling, and expert CA review when the case needs judgment.

The brand line for campaign and page planning is:

> Smart tax solutions with expert-backed filing, document clarity, and compliance confidence.

Use this line as a strategic anchor, not as mandatory copy on every page. The stronger everyday language is practical: scope, documents, checks, timelines, review, filing path, reconciliation, and next step.

## 2. Audience And Intent

Write for people who want tax work to feel understandable before they pay or upload documents. The main audiences are:

- Salaried taxpayers preparing Form 16, AIS, Form 26AS, deductions, refund, and ITR form choices.
- Freelancers and professionals managing client receipts, TDS, GST, presumptive-tax questions, and business income.
- Investors with capital gains, broker statements, RSU/ESOP, foreign assets, or tax-credit mismatch risk.
- Startups and small businesses needing GST, TDS, registrations, ROC, payroll, accounting, and virtual CFO support.
- Cautious users who need trust, privacy, refund boundaries, document handling, and CA review expectations before acting.

The emotional goal is relief through clarity. The user should feel: "I know what to prepare, what MyeCA will help with, what is excluded, and when an expert should review this."

## 3. Website Content Direction

### Homepage

The first screen should make the offer understandable within five seconds:

- What MyeCA does: ITR filing, tax calculators, GST, TDS, notices, documents, and business compliance.
- Who it helps: individuals, freelancers, investors, startups, and businesses.
- Why it is safer: secure documents, scope before payment, and expert support.
- What to do next: `Start Filing Now`, `Free Tax Calculator`, `Compare Pricing`, or `Talk to Expert`.

Homepage copy should continue to support AY 2026-27 ITR-season conversion with expert eCA assistance, free notice assistance, secure document handling, and a clear `Start Filing Now` path.

### Primary Journeys

Keep public navigation language simple and intent-based:

- `File ITR`
- `Use Calculator`
- `Compare Pricing`
- `Read Blogs`
- `Trust/Security`
- `Contact/Expert Consultation`

Secondary navigation can include specific tools such as `Income Tax Calculator`, `Regime Compare`, `Parse Form 16`, `Notice Help`, and `Document Vault`.

### Service Pages

Every service page should answer the user's practical questions before asking for payment:

- Who the service is for.
- What documents are needed.
- What the expected timeline is.
- What the price/scope signal is.
- What is included and excluded.
- What can delay the work.
- When expert CA review is recommended.
- What happens after the user submits the request.

Use scannable blocks: document lists, timeline blocks, included/excluded tables, escalation triggers, and short FAQs.

### Blog And Content Hub

Blogs should be educational but conversion-aware. A reader should never reach the end without a practical next step.

Each article should connect to one of these outcomes:

- Simple case: filing flow or ITR form selector.
- Calculation need: calculator or parser.
- Document-heavy case: document vault or checklist.
- Complex case: expert consultation.
- Research mode: related guide, category hub, or comparison page.

For tax and legal topics, content must separate education from professional advice. This matters most for refunds, notices, capital gains, foreign assets, business income, AIS/Form 26AS mismatches, and Income Tax Act transition topics.

### Trust Pages

Trust content should be visible before users upload documents. Cover:

- Privacy and secure document handling.
- Scope clarity before payment.
- Refund and processing boundaries.
- CA review expectations.
- What MyeCA can and cannot promise.
- How uploaded documents support review, filing, notices, and future records.

### Mobile Content

Mobile pages should use short summaries, tight cards, and obvious next actions. Prioritize:

- `File ITR`
- `Income Tax Calculator`
- `Parse Form 16`
- `Notice Help`
- `Compare Pricing`
- `Talk to Expert`

Avoid long hero paragraphs on mobile. Put the decision path first, then supporting proof.

## 4. Voice And Copy Rules

The voice is calm, practical, evidence-first, and expert-backed. Avoid generic fintech excitement.

Preferred wording:

- `review-ready`
- `document-backed`
- `case-specific`
- `filing path`
- `reconciliation`
- `expert-assisted`
- `before submission`
- `scope shown upfront`
- `document path`
- `review level`
- `official portal`
- `working file`

Avoid weak or risky language:

- Do not promise refunds, tax savings, notice immunity, fastest processing, or guaranteed approval.
- Do not imply calculator output is final filing advice.
- Do not present educational content as a CA opinion.
- Do not imply every user needs paid CA help.
- Do not overstate AI or automation. Position tools as preparation and review support.

Preferred content pattern:

1. State the user's situation.
2. Name the records or facts to check.
3. Explain the decision or risk.
4. Show the next step.
5. Route simple cases to self-service and complex cases to expert review.

Example:

> If your Form 16, AIS, and Form 26AS match cleanly, start with the guided filing path. If capital gains, foreign assets, business income, or a notice is involved, request case-specific CA review before submission.

## 5. Color Design And Theme

MyeCA should remain a clean, professional, calm, document-focused, modern Indian compliance platform.

The approved visual foundation is mostly blue, white, slate, and light-blue surfaces with green for trust, completion, secure-document, and verified states.

### Primary Palette

| Role | Token | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary brand blue | `#315efb` | Links, highlights, active nav states, category labels, informational CTAs |
| Authority blue | `#2040d8` | Hover states, stronger trust moments, active button states |
| Deep blue | `#0646b2` | Hero emphasis, stronger headers, high-confidence informational surfaces |
| Trust green | `#047857` | Success states, secure document cues, verified checklist items |
| Fresh trust green | `#00a86b` | Completion markers, positive campaign badges, filing availability accents |
| Soft surface | `#F8FAFC` | Public section backgrounds and calm content bands |
| Light brand surface | `#EEF4FF` | Blue-tinted information surfaces and hero support areas |

### Accent Rules

- Use orange or amber only for notices, deadlines, urgency, and support categories.
- Use red only for errors, risk warnings, and destructive states.
- Use green for positive proof, document security, verified states, completion, and the ITR filing stamp.
- Avoid loud gradients, neon accents, decorative blobs, oversized claims, or playful visual language.
- Keep cards and sections quiet. Public pages can breathe, but operational tools should stay compact.

## 6. Typography And Layout

MyeCA uses Inter as the UI font. Keep the existing type-role system:

- Use hero-scale type only for true first-viewport heroes and article titles.
- Use tighter page titles for service pages, calculators, ITR flows, parsers, auth pages, dashboards, and tools.
- Use section titles for lower public sections.
- Use card titles for cards, panels, and compact summary blocks.
- Use article prose for blogs, legal pages, and help content.

Layout should help users scan:

- Short paragraphs.
- Checklist blocks.
- Comparison tables.
- Document lists.
- "When to get expert help" sections.
- Clear CTAs tied to the current intent.

Do not turn operational pages into landing pages. Calculators, parsers, ITR flows, and dashboards should feel useful immediately.

## 7. Content Production Rules

### Tax And Legal Articles

Every tax or legal article should include:

- Relevant FY, AY, tax period, or effective date.
- Audience.
- Document checklist.
- Official-source links where applicable.
- Risk caveats.
- Practical next step.
- CTA that matches intent.

Recommended article structure:

1. What this guide covers.
2. Who it is for.
3. Year or period clarification.
4. Documents and records to collect.
5. Decision framework.
6. Common risks or mistakes.
7. When to get expert help.
8. Practical next step.

### Service Pages

Every service page should include:

- Audience.
- Scope.
- Documents.
- Timeline.
- Pricing signal or price/scope note.
- Exclusions.
- Escalation triggers.
- Expert review route.
- Related guides or calculators.

### Calculator And Tool Pages

Every calculator or tool page should say the output is an estimate, planning aid, or preparation tool. It should explain what records the user should verify before filing or paying.

Use CTAs this way:

- Simple filing result: `Start ITR filing` or `Continue filing`.
- Uncertain filing result: `Ask CA before paying`.
- Research result: `Read guide` or `Compare regimes`.
- Document-heavy result: `Prepare documents` or `Open document checklist`.

### Navigation And Brand

Use simple labels:

- `Blogs`
- `Pricing`
- `Trust`
- `Contact`
- `File ITR`
- `Calculators`
- `Notice Help`

Reuse the MyeCA brand lockup and the `SMART TAX SOLUTIONS` treatment. Do not create alternate logo systems, alternate taglines, or campaign-only wordmarks without a separate brand decision.

## 9. Page Family Guidance

| Page family | Goal | Content style | CTA style |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | Explain offer fast | Short, confident, proof-backed | Start filing, calculator, pricing, expert help |
| Service pages | Convert qualified users | Scope-first, document-heavy | Request scoped review, compare pricing |
| Blog pages | Educate and route | Evidence-first, year-specific | Tool, filing path, expert review, related guide |
| Calculator pages | Help decide next step | Estimate caveat plus action | File with estimate, ask CA, read guide |
| Trust pages | Reduce hesitation | Plain, transparent, non-salesy | Review privacy, continue to filing |
| Contact pages | Route support | Clear reasons and service intent | Request callback, expert consultation |

## 10. Acceptance Checklist For Content Review

Before publishing a page, check:

- A first-time visitor can understand what MyeCA does, who it helps, and where to start.
- A cautious taxpayer can find trust, privacy, pricing, and document-handling information before uploading.
- A complex filer can recognize when expert CA review is recommended.
- A blog reader has a relevant next action.
- The page uses mostly blue, white, slate, and light-blue surfaces.
- Green is reserved for trust, secure document handling, verification, and completion.
- Orange/amber is reserved for notices, deadlines, urgency, or support categories.
- Red is reserved for risk, error, or destructive action.
- The page does not make unsafe tax, refund, approval, savings, or notice outcome promises.
- The CTA matches the user's intent and risk level.
