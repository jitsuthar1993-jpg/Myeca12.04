Draft:

```text
AY 2026-27 filing season is easier when the first step is document readiness, not choosing a platform in a hurry.

Before starting an ITR, salaried taxpayers should keep Form 16, AIS, Form 26AS, bank interest details, deduction proofs, capital gains statements, rent or home-loan documents, and refund bank details together. The important check is not just whether the documents exist. It is whether the numbers agree with each other.

Five checks before filing:

1. Match salary and TDS in Form 16 with Form 26AS.
2. Review AIS for bank interest, securities transactions, dividends, rent, and other income categories.
3. Compare old and new regime results before assuming last year's choice is still best.
4. Prepare broker profit and loss reports early if there are shares, mutual funds, or VDA transactions.
5. Keep evidence for deductions and exemptions in one folder before starting the return.

MyeCA has a practical AY 2026-27 ITR season hub with checklists for AIS mismatches, Form 16 preparation, capital gains statements, and refund-status tracking:

This is educational guidance. A CA review is still useful where facts are complex, income is split across multiple sources, or the taxpayer has notices, foreign assets, capital gains, business income, or mismatch risk.
```
