# AY 2026-27 ITR Paid Content Funnel

## Conversion Spine

Primary conversion: paid ITR filing start.

Secondary conversion: expert consultation for complex cases.

Campaign window: 90 days from June 2026 through the main AY 2026-27 filing season.

Budget: INR 200,000 monthly paid budget, split across high-intent search, retargeting, Meta retargeting, and LinkedIn/YouTube tests. Use `docs/marketing/itr-season-2026-paid-media-plan.csv` as the execution sheet.

Core message: File your ITR confidently with guided workflows, clear scope before payment, and optional CA assistance.

## Source And Claim Discipline

Official and quality references:

- Income Tax e-Filing portal: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/
- Income Tax Returns FAQ: https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/%20income%20tax%20returns-faq
- Google Search Central helpful content guidance: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- Google SEO Starter Guide: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide

Operating rule: every promoted page must stay educational, reviewed, and source-aware. Avoid refund promises, notice-outcome promises, fastest-processing claims, government-affiliation wording, unqualified paid backlinks, and exact-match anchor manipulation.

The launch urgency is real: the Income Tax e-Filing portal home page showed ITR-1, ITR-2, and ITR-4 for AY 2026-27 live on May 31, 2026. Re-check the live portal before major ad launches because filing utilities and portal notices can change during the season.

## Audience And Pillars

Primary audiences:

- Salaried filers and first-time filers.
- Job switchers and multiple Form 16 users.
- Form 16 and AIS/Form 26AS mismatch cases.
- Refund seekers and notice-risk users.
- Capital-gains, F&O, crypto, freelancer, NRI, and foreign-asset cases.

Content pillars:

- Filing readiness: ITR forms, deadlines, documents, Form 16, old/new regime, and form selector.
- Risk and correction: AIS mismatch, defective return, refund delay, notices, and missed deductions.
- Complex filing: capital gains, F&O, crypto/VDA, freelance income, NRI, and foreign assets.
- Tool-led education: Form 16 parser, income-tax calculator, regime comparator, and capital-gains import.
- Trust and comparison: CA-assisted versus self-prepared filing, pricing explainers, MyeCA workflow, and review scope.

## Content Execution

Weeks 1-2:

- Refresh one high-intent ITR page per working day before writing lower-intent posts.
- Publish short answers around filing start, Form 16, AIS mismatch, refund status, old/new regime, and form selection.
- Add tool CTAs to every promoted page: one calculator/tool, one paid filing path, one expert-review path where the facts are complex.

Weeks 3-6:

- Publish or refresh five assets per week.
- Build one outreach-ready asset per week for HR/payroll, finance creators, CA communities, or taxpayer communities.
- Use Search Console query growth to decide refreshes before new topics.

Weeks 7-13:

- Shift spend toward pages with proven tool-start and paid-filing-start conversion.
- Consolidate thin or overlapping articles into stronger hub guides.
- Retarget visitors who used a tool but did not start paid filing.

## Paid Amplification Rules

Use paid only where the next action is obvious.

- Google Search captures filing-intent users and sends them to `/itr/form-selector`, `/form16-parser`, `/calculators/income-tax`, and asset pages.
- Remarketing brings back blog, checklist, and tool visitors who have not started filing.
- Meta retargeting uses checklist and deadline reminders for warm visitors.
- LinkedIn/YouTube tests reach salaried, founder, freelancer, and professional segments with short educational explainers.

Negative keyword themes: free-only, government login-only, refund promise, jobs, PDF-only, unrelated GST-only, and non-India queries.

## Lead Magnets And Nurture

Lead magnets:

- AY 2026-27 document checklist.
- AIS/Form 26AS mismatch checklist.
- Capital gains broker statement checklist.
- Refund and deadline tracker.

Nurture sequence:

- Day 0: deliver checklist and route to the highest-fit tool.
- Day 2: calculator or parser follow-up.
- Day 4: filing-risk guide and mismatch review prompt.
- Day 6: pricing and scope-before-payment explanation.
- Day 10: deadline reminder and paid filing CTA.

## Measurement

Weekly reporting uses `docs/marketing/itr-season-2026-weekly-kpi-template.csv`.

Track:

- Attract: organic impressions, organic CTR, paid CTR, paid CPC, top query cluster.
- Engage: tool starts, checklist downloads, WhatsApp/email opt-ins, returning users.
- Convert: paid filing starts, paid CPA, content-to-tool conversion, tool-to-paid conversion, expert consultation requests.

Do not call the broader campaign complete until owner-side ad account, Search Console, Bing, WhatsApp/email, and live placement evidence are recorded. Repo-side readiness means the assets, routing, and checks exist; account-side performance proof is separate.
