# MyeCA Site Claim Audit

Last updated: 2026-05-25

This audit lists public claims that must stay conservative unless MyeCA has current proof, policy text, or implementation evidence. The rewrite policy is: scope-first pricing, credential-checked professionals, no unsupported ratings or success guarantees, estimates instead of assured outcomes, and security wording limited to controls supported by the current app.

| ID | Claim area | Current risk | Decision | Replacement direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C01 | Homepage ITR filing promise | Medium | Keep with qualification | Say guided AY 2026-27 filing with optional or applicable CA-assisted review. |
| C02 | ITR pricing | High | Scope-first | Simple salary may show starts/fixed entry price; complex returns need scope review or quote. |
| C03 | CA review availability | High | Qualify | Use CA-assisted review where applicable, not every return or every plan. |
| C04 | CA credentials | High | Soften | Use credential-checked tax professionals unless exact ICAI/licensing proof is supplied. |
| C05 | AIS/26AS checks | Medium | Keep with scope | Say available in assisted review workflows after document intake. |
| C06 | Tax savings and notice outcomes | High | Remove guarantees | Replace maximum savings, error-free, no-notice wording with eligible deduction review and issue flags. |
| C07 | Document scanner extraction | High | Assistive only | Say supported extraction helps organize fields and requires user or expert review. |
| C08 | Document security | High | Code-supported only | Claim authenticated/private access, secure transport, scoped access, and user-managed delete where implemented. |
| C09 | Support/callback | Medium | Business-hours | Say requests are reviewed during business hours and next steps depend on scope/readiness. |
| C10 | Ratings and review counts | High | Remove | Do not publish aggregate ratings/counts without proof and consented data. |
| C11 | GST timelines | Medium | Estimate | Use approximate, department-dependent timelines. |
| C12 | Registration approvals/success | High | Remove guarantees | No first-time approval, success rate, or approval guarantee claims. |
| C13 | MSME/DPIIT benefits | Medium | Qualify | Benefits are subject to eligibility and government rules. |
| C14 | Refund promises | Medium | Policy only | Keep refund eligibility only on the refund policy page. |

| C16 | Calculator freshness/accuracy | Medium | Estimate | Say calculators provide estimates for the current assessment-year assumptions and need review. |
| C17 | Testimonials | High | Anonymized only | Use anonymized workflow examples, not star ratings or public endorsements. |
