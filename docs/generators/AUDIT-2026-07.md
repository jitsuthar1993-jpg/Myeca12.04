# Document generator legal, print, and usability audit

Audit date: 2026-07-17
Scope: all 60 entries in `client/src/data/generator-registry.json`, their loader modules, form schemas, HTML generators, preview paginator, and PDF/Word export path.

## Executive conclusion

The generator catalogue is fully wired: all 60 registry IDs have a loader and exported config, and the existing focused suite confirms that advertised previews render without throwing. The catalogue should not, however, be described as “legally compliant” as a whole. It contains three different products:

1. statutory/compliance-shaped forms and tax documents;
2. contractual/legal drafts that require facts, state-specific stamp/registration treatment, execution, and professional review; and
3. general letters, certificates, internal records, resumes, and financial statements whose validity comes from the issuing person or institution, not from the template alone.

The most important product risk was the export pipeline: PDF printing previously wrote raw generator HTML into a new window with no shared A4 margins, repeatable table headers, orphan/widow control, or keep-together rules. The shared print wrapper now supplies those rules, and the main document families opt into the same document class. The remaining legal gaps are document-specific and are listed below.

## Evidence and legal boundary

This is a product and template audit, not a legal opinion. A generated document is a draft unless the relevant authority, parties, witnesses, notary, stamp authority, registrar, employer, institution, or tax system accepts it.

- GST tax invoices must contain the particulars prescribed by CGST Rule 46, including supplier/recipient identity, serial number, date, HSN/SAC or accounting code where applicable, taxable value, tax rate and amount, place of supply for inter-State supplies, and other applicable particulars. See the [CBIC GST invoice rules](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/gst-invoice-rules.html) and [CGST Rules PDF, Rule 46](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/cgst-rules-30122017.pdf).
- A lease exceeding one year, from year to year, or reserving yearly rent generally requires registration under the Registration Act, 1908; state tenancy and leave-and-license rules can be stricter. See [India Code, Registration Act section 17](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/show-data?actid=AC_CEN_18_43_00004_190816_1523340837338&orderno=18&sectionId=37616&sectionno=17) and the [state-specific example for Maharashtra](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/18914/1/a1908-16.pdf).
- Form 15G/15H must follow the current Income Tax Department form and eligibility instructions; a visually similar self-declaration must not be presented as the official electronic filing. See the [Income Tax Department forms guidance](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/income-tax-forms?mobile-app=1).

## Catalogue inventory and required legal/content review

Status meanings: **shared-ready** means the common print/export/usability improvements apply; **document review required** means the generator still needs a fact-specific legal/content pass before being marketed as complete; **statutory-sensitive** means the official form/rule or state/authority requirements must be checked at release time.

| Family | Generators | Review result |
|---|---|---|
| Residential and commercial property | `rent-agreement-rc`, `rent-agreement-comm`, `leave-license`, `lease-deed`, `rent-receipt`, `society-noc`, `possession-letter` | **Document review required.** Add state, property identity, parties' KYC, rent/deposit/escalation, term/renewal, utilities/repairs, possession/termination, notices, witnesses, execution and stamp/registration guidance. Do not imply an 11-month or unregistered draft is universally sufficient. |
| Affidavits and declarations | `affidavit-name`, `affidavit-address`, `general-affidavit`, `one-same-person-affidavit`, `huf-affidavit`, `msme-decl` | **Document review required.** Require identity/address, age and relationship, numbered facts, verification/declaration, deponent signature, date/place, witnesses/notary/oath commissioner, stamp-paper guidance, and receiving-authority limitations. HUF and Udyam declarations need fact-specific and portal-specific checks. |
| Authority and transfer instruments | `poa-general`, `poa-special`, `gift-deed`, `relinquishment-deed`, `will`, `promissory-note`, `loan-agreement` | **High legal risk; document review required.** Add capacity/identity, consideration or gift intent, property/asset schedule, revocation/default, indemnity, governing law, execution/witness clauses, and registration/stamp guidance. A Will must include testamentary capacity, revocation, executor, beneficiary/asset clarity, attestation and safe-execution guidance; a Power of Attorney must not imply powers beyond the stated scope. |
| Corporate agreements | `contract-service`, `contract-nda`, `llp-agreement`, `partnership-deed`, `founder-agreement`, `board-resolution-bank`, `board-resolution-gst`, `gst-auth` | **High legal risk; document review required.** Add legal entity details, authority/capacity, definitions, term/termination, payment/tax, IP, confidentiality/exceptions, liability/indemnity, dispute resolution, notices, signatures and board/partner approval details. LLP/partnership/company requirements vary by constitution and state/registrar. |
| GST/commercial documents | `invoice`, `gst-quotation`, `proforma-invoice`, `purchase-order`, `delivery-challan`, `payment-receipt`, `gst-credit-debit-note`, `expense-reimbursement` | **Statutory-sensitive.** Keep the non-tax labels on quotations, proformas and POs; implement rule-based invoice/credit-note validation before calling the output GST compliant; require supplier GSTIN, recipient status/GSTIN, serial series, dates, place of supply/state codes, HSN/SAC, tax treatment, reverse-charge flag where applicable, totals and linked original invoice details for notes. |
| Financial statements | `msme-cash-flow`, `projected-balance-sheet`, `net-worth-statement`, `report` | **Uncertified financial statement.** Require period basis, accounting basis, assumptions, source/supporting documents, preparer identity, signatory and an explicit unaudited/self-prepared label. Block export when balance or required inputs fail validation. |
| Income-tax declarations | `form-15g`, `form-15h`, `form-12bb` | **Statutory-sensitive.** Track assessment/financial year and official form version; validate PAN, age, estimated income, tax computation and declaration fields; distinguish printable employer/collector submission from portal filing. Never claim that a generated page is an official filed form. |
| Employment/HR | `offer-letter`, `experience-letter`, `salary-slip`, `warning-letter`, `certificate`, `resume` | **Issuer-controlled.** Require employer/institution identity, employee identifiers, dates, designation, remuneration components and authorised signatory; prevent salary-slip totals from being presented as payroll records unless verified by the issuer. |
| Public applications and notices | `legal-notice`, `rti-application`, `consumer-complaint-letter`, `police-complaint-lost-document`, `pension-request-application`, `noc`, `invitation-letter` | **Authority-specific.** Require recipient authority/address, jurisdiction, facts, relief/request, enclosures, contact details, signature and acknowledgement instructions. RTI must retain Section 6(1) framing and fee/channel caveats; notices need limitation/jurisdiction/professional review warnings. |
| Institutional and personal documents | `bonafide-certificate`, `transfer-certificate`, `student-fee-receipt`, `marriage-biodata` | **Issuer-controlled/general draft.** Clearly identify the issuing institution or applicant, document number/date, verification/contact details, signature/seal placeholder, and avoid representing a self-generated certificate as an institutional record. |

## Cross-cutting findings

### Legal completeness

- **P0:** No single template can be declared legally complete across India. State stamp duty, registration, tenancy, notarial, institutional, and authority rules are not encoded in the common generator contract.
- **P1:** Statutory-shaped documents need version metadata and an explicit “last checked” source/authority before their title can contain words such as “compliant”, “official”, or “Form”.
- **P1:** Required input schemas are inconsistent. Some fields are optional where the output needs them to be meaningful, and many generators do not expose witnesses, execution, verification, enclosures, signatory capacity, or governing authority.
- **P1:** Legal drafts need a visible, per-document limitations notice and a CA/lawyer/notary/authority review path appropriate to the document family.

### Print, spacing, and next-page behavior

- The shared A4 wrapper now sets print margins, typography defaults, overflow wrapping, repeated table headers, row/heading keep-together rules, orphan/widow control, and explicit page-break classes.
- The preview paginator now splits transaction tables by body row and retains the table header on each preview page. Long single paragraphs or deeply nested non-table blocks should still be checked with rendered PDFs; no browser paginator can guarantee perfect semantic pagination for arbitrary user text.
- Tables should use `thead`, avoid fixed widths that exceed 178 mm, and keep totals/signatures together. Financial and transaction factories now opt into the shared document class; simple-letter forms use reduced inner padding and responsive one-column fields on small screens.

### Usability and accessibility

- All field labels should be associated with controls, errors should be shown next to the relevant control, and required fields should be visibly marked. This is not yet uniform across every custom form.
- Long forms need sections, clear required/optional language, stable add/remove controls, keyboard-accessible actions, and a mobile preview that does not force an unreadable desktop-width canvas.
- Export should wait for the print window to load and should report popup blocking. The shared export path now does this and preserves the compliance notice in PDF/Word output.

## Remediation backlog

1. Create a typed `DocumentLegalProfile` for every registry entry: document class, statutory source, jurisdiction, version date, required fields, execution requirements, limitations, and reviewer.
2. Add schema tests that assert each profile’s required output points are present in generated HTML and that missing critical facts block export.
3. Replace the legacy standalone invoice implementation with the shared financial transaction implementation or remove the unused duplicate; maintain one source of truth for GST calculations and escaping.
4. Add generated PDF fixtures for one representative document from each family with short, long, empty-optional, multi-item, and multi-page data. Inspect every fixture visually at 100% and 200% zoom.
5. Add Playwright checks for: registry-to-editor navigation, mobile form entry, preview open/close, add/remove line items, long-text pagination, popup-blocked export, and download/print handoff.
6. Add current official-source review dates for Rule 46, Form 15G/15H, Form 12BB, RTI, and state-specific property templates before a release can use “compliant” in public copy.

## Release gate

Do not release a generator as legally sufficient until its legal profile, schema tests, generated-content checks, rendered PDF review, mobile browser test, and named reviewer are all complete. Until then, label outputs as drafts and direct users to the relevant authority or qualified professional for execution and filing.
